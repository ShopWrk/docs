---
title: "Schedule and manage jobs from estimate to completion"
description: "Turn an approved estimate into a scheduled job, edit services and discounts for that job, assign your team, and finish the work before you invoice."
---

A job is the work you are doing on a vehicle. Most jobs start when a customer approves an estimate. You can also create a job from the calendar. After that you schedule it, assign people, track progress, and mark it complete before you send an invoice.

## How estimates become jobs

When a customer approves an estimate, open the estimate and click **Convert to Job**. You will see the customer, vehicle, and services so you can confirm them.

You can set the date, time, technician, and bay now, or leave them blank and schedule later. Click **Convert to Job** to create the job and open its detail page.

If the estimate had a discount, that discount comes with the job. You can change it later when you edit the job.

<Note>
  You can also create a job from the calendar with no estimate. Open the calendar, click **New Job**, and fill in the customer, vehicle, and services.
</Note>

## Job statuses

Jobs move through these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Booked with a date and time |
| **In Progress** | Work has started |
| **Pending Review** | Work is done and waiting on a quality check |
| **Completed** | Finished and ready to invoice |
| **Cancelled** | Stopped before completion |

Change the status from the dropdown at the top of the overview tab on the job page.

## Scheduling a job

Open the job and set the scheduled date and time. Jobs with a date show on the **Calendar**, where you can see work across bays and technicians.

If an overnight or after-hours time looks off for your shop hours, hover the warning on the schedule. It is guidance, not a hard block.

<Tip>
  If Google Calendar is connected, jobs with a date and time show up there too.
</Tip>

## Assigning technicians and bays

You can assign people two ways:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Per job">
    On the job detail page, set the primary technician and bay for the whole job. That is what the calendar shows.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Per service">
    In **Services** on the job overview, each service line has its own technician. Use that when one tech does tint and another does PPF on the same car.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Edit services on a job

If one car needs a different price or wording than your usual list, change it on the job. Your services list in Settings stays the same.

Open the job and click **Edit**. You can also set this while creating a job from the calendar. Completed and cancelled jobs stay locked, so edit before you mark the job complete.

Open a service under **Services** and change:

- Name
- Description
- Duration
- Price

Click **Done** to save that line. Only this job keeps the new values.

Checklists still sit under each service. Editing the line does not remove them.

Scheduling uses the duration you saved on the job, so a longer custom service blocks the right amount of time on the calendar.

## Apply a discount to a job

If you want to knock money off the whole job, use the same discount controls you already use on estimates and invoices.

On create or edit, turn on **Discount**. Choose a fixed dollar amount or a percentage. The job total updates right away. Tax and balance due follow that discounted total.

You can turn the discount off later, or switch between dollar and percent. The total is discounted once, not twice.

Completed and cancelled jobs cannot take a new discount. Change it while the job is still open.

## Tracking job progress

The overview tab is where you watch the job:

- **Status dropdown** to move the job forward
- **Services list** with technicians and the edited prices
- **Customer notes** that the customer can see in the portal
- **Internal notes** for staff only
- **Materials** when inventory tracking is on

### Checklists

Once the job is **In Progress**, checklists for this job type show at the bottom of Notes. Techs check items off as they go. Customers do not see checklists.

## Pricing, deposit, and payments

The right column on overview shows:

- **Subtotal, discount, tax, and total** from the services and discount on this job
- **Deposit status** for how much is collected versus required
- **Send invoice** to open a new invoice with this job's customer and services

If the estimate required a deposit, you can send a deposit request by email or text from the job page.

When you send the invoice, it picks up the current service lines and discount from the job.

## Marking a job complete

When the work is done, set the status to **Completed**. Then click **Send invoice** in the pricing card.

<Warning>
  Deleting a job cannot be undone. The **Delete** button is at the top right. Only delete a job if you are sure it should not be invoiced or kept.
</Warning>
