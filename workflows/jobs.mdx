A job is the actual work on a vehicle: what gets installed, who installs it, when it happens, and whether it got done right. Most jobs start life automatically the moment a customer approves an estimate, but you can also book one from scratch on the calendar.

## Where jobs come from

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="From an approved estimate" icon="file-check">
    When a customer approves an estimate and pays the deposit, ShopWrk creates the job automatically with everything carried over.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From the calendar" icon="calendar-plus">
    Click any day on the calendar and choose New Job to book work that never had an estimate, like a walk-in.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From a booking request" icon="inbox">
    When a customer converts an accepted booking request, ShopWrk creates the job linked to the contact. Schedule it on the calendar.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a job from the calendar

Open **Calendar**, click **New Job** (or click a day), and the two-screen builder opens:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Details">
    Pick the customer and vehicle, add services from your catalog, set the date, time, and bay, and assign a technician.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    The second screen shows the full picture: services with tier-based pricing, totals, and schedule. Fix anything that looks wrong, then create the job.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new job appears on the calendar immediately. Jobs created without a date land in the **Waiting** column so they never get forgotten; drag one onto a day whenever a slot opens.

## Job statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Created but not yet scheduled |
| **Scheduled** | Booked with a date, time, bay, and technician |
| **In Progress** | Work has started on the vehicle |
| **Pending Review** | Technician finished and submitted; awaiting owner or manager sign-off |
| **Completed** | Approved and ready for invoicing |
| **Cancelled** | Not happening; kept for records |
| **Rescheduled** | Moved to a new date |

Change status from the dropdown at the top of the job page. Pending Review is the quality gate: the technician signals they are done, and someone with approval rights checks photos and checklist items before it flips to Completed. Shops that skip the gate can approve straight through.

## Assigning technicians and bays

Assignment works at two levels:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Whole job">
    Set the primary technician and bay on the job itself. This is what the calendar shows.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Per service">
    Each service line has its own technician selector. Use it when one tech does the tint and another handles the PPF on the same car. Per-service assignments also drive commissions once compensation is configured.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Running the day on a job

The job overview keeps everything in one place:

- Services with assigned techs and pricing, pulled from the original quote
- Customer-facing notes and internal notes, clearly separated
- Checklists the technician works through while the job is In Progress
- Consumed materials, if you track inventory
- Photos, organized as Before, During, After, and Damage

The Damage category exists for a reason. Photograph anything questionable before touching the panel, and the record protects both the shop and the customer.

## Deposits, payments, and invoicing

If the job came from an approved estimate, the deposit is already collected and shows in the pricing card, deducted from the balance. From there:

1. Mark the job **Completed**.
2. Click **Create invoice**. Review the services, the deposit credit, tax, and the remaining balance.
3. Create and send it. The customer pays through their [payment link](/integrations/shopwrk-pay).

For jobs created without an estimate, you can request a deposit from the job page before work starts, by email or SMS.

<Note>
  If you connect Google Calendar, scheduled jobs appear on the connected calendar automatically. Make job and assignment changes in ShopWrk so the linked Google events stay accurate.
</Note>
