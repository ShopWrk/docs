Estimates let you quote a customer before work starts. Build the estimate in a step-by-step wizard, choose how package tiers are structured, then send a link the customer can review and approve on their own device. If you turn on **Collect deposit on approval**, they can pay the deposit through [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay) in that same public flow.

## Creating a new estimate

Go to **Estimates** in the sidebar and click **New Estimate**. The wizard walks through four steps: customer, vehicle, services, and pricing review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a customer">
    Search by name, phone, or email. If the customer is new, click **Add new customer** from the search panel without leaving the wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a vehicle">
    Choose a vehicle on file, or click **Add new vehicle** for year, make, model, and color. ShopWrk uses the vehicle to fill the right size prices from your catalog (sedan, SUV, or truck).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add services">
    Browse your catalog by category. Select packages and services. Each service can show package-tier pricing. The price also follows the vehicle size from the previous step.

    <Tip>
      You can override the auto-detected vehicle size in the services step using the selector at the top of the panel.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review pricing and send">
    Set a discount (fixed dollar or percentage) and a tax rate. Turn on **Collect deposit on approval** if you want the customer to pay the deposit when they approve. The deposit amount comes from your service settings, not a number you type on the estimate. Choose email, SMS, or both, then click **Create Estimate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier-based pricing (Good / Better / Best)

ShopWrk supports multi-tier estimates where the customer picks a package level, typically **Good**, **Better**, and **Best**, and sees a different price for each. That is the default for new estimates.

Good / Better / Best are package levels the customer picks. Separately, ShopWrk prices each level by vehicle size (sedan, SUV, truck) from your catalog. The wizard uses the vehicle you selected to fill the right size prices.

| Vehicle type | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Sedan | Cars and smaller coupes |
| SUV | SUVs, crossovers, and minivans |
| Truck | Full-size trucks |

When you add a service with tier pricing enabled, the matching per-size price comes from your catalog. You can still adjust any line item before sending.

<Note>
  For a flat single-price estimate with no package choice, turn off **Use tiers** in the services step. Line items then use a single price.
</Note>

## Sending the estimate to a customer

From the estimate detail page, click **Send**. Deliver by:

- **Email.** Branded email with a unique link.
- **SMS.** Text with the same link.

The customer does not need a ShopWrk account. You can resend anytime from the detail page.

## Edit an estimate after sending

Open the estimate and click **Edit**. You can update an estimate that is sent, viewed, declined, expired, or approved. Save, then resend by email, SMS, or both.

The customer's existing link opens the latest saved version. If they still have an older tab open, ShopWrk asks them to refresh before they approve or decline.

Editing an approved estimate removes the current approval. The customer must review and approve again. ShopWrk keeps the version they approved before your edit.

<Note>
  You cannot edit an estimate after it has been converted to a job. Update the job or invoice instead.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Set a **Valid until** date so approval expires after that date. Expired estimates show a notice to the customer.
</Tip>

## What the customer sees

When the customer opens their estimate link, they see:

- Your shop branding and the services quoted
- Package options (if tiers are on). They pick **Good**, **Better**, or **Best**
- Optional add-ons as checkboxes
- A live price summary that updates with their selections
- **Approve** and **Decline** buttons

If **Collect deposit on approval** is on, approve takes them into [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay) for the deposit. They approve and pay in one flow.

If a contract must be signed before approval, they are prompted to sign first.

They can also send a question from the estimate page. Questions land in your messaging inbox.

## Approving, paying the deposit, and converting

Once the customer clicks **Approve**, the estimate status becomes **Approved** in your dashboard.

If **Collect deposit on approval** is on, ShopWrk creates an unscheduled job and that job's invoice before taking money. The deposit is a payment request on that invoice. Success shows the approved work, the linked job, deposit paid, and that the shop will contact them to schedule.

If they leave before paying, the approval still stands. The job and invoice stay unpaid. You can send the pay link again. See [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay).

Send the remaining balance later on that same invoice. See [Invoices and payments](/workflows/invoices-payments).

If **Collect deposit on approval** is off, approval only accepts the quote. Open the approved estimate and click **Convert to Job**. On conversion you can optionally set date and time, technician, and bay. All of those fields are optional.

If Collect deposit on approval already created the job, schedule that job. Do not convert the estimate again.

<Note>
  You can only convert an estimate once. After conversion, the estimate shows a link to the job it created.
</Note>

If the customer declines, they can leave a reason. Status becomes **Declined** and the reason shows on the estimate detail page.

<Warning>
  A customer cannot approve an expired estimate. After the valid-until date they see an expiry notice and cannot proceed.
</Warning>
