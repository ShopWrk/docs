After creating your account, ShopWrk guides you through a short setup wizard before taking you to your dashboard. The wizard collects your business name, location, the services you offer, and the size of your team. After the wizard, you'll see a feature showcase and pick your theme before being introduced to your AI assistant, Shopwrker.

If you leave mid-flow, ShopWrk saves your progress automatically. Return to the onboarding URL and you'll resume at the last incomplete step.

## The setup wizard

The wizard has five steps. Each one takes under a minute to complete.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your shop name">
    The first step asks you to confirm or set your shop's name.

    You can type the name directly, or use the **Find your business on Google** search to look up your shop. If ShopWrk finds a matching Google Business Profile, it prefills your name, address, phone number, and business hours automatically, which saves you work in the steps that follow.

    <Tip>
      Searching for your business on Google at this step is optional, but it can cut the rest of the wizard down to a quick review rather than manual data entry.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your shop's location">
    Enter your shop's street address, city, state or province, and postal code. ShopWrk uses this to configure your booking page, invoices, and local service settings.

    If the welcome step pulled your address from a Google Business Profile, this step is skipped automatically and you're taken directly to the Confirm step to review what was imported.

    <Note>
      ShopWrk supports US and Canadian addresses. Select the correct country from the dropdown so the postal code format validates correctly.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your shop details">
    This step shows a summary of what ShopWrk has collected so far: your shop name, address, phone number, website, and business hours.

    Review each field and edit anything that looks wrong. If you imported data from Google, this is the place to correct any details that didn't come through accurately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what you offer">
    Select all the services your shop provides. ShopWrk uses this to configure your service catalog and set up relevant defaults.

    Available service types:

    | Service | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Window Tint** | Films, ceramic tints |
    | **Paint Protection Film** | Clear bras, full PPF |
    | **Ceramic Coating** | 9H coatings, graphene |
    | **Vinyl Wrap** | Color change, partial wraps |
    | **Detailing** | Interior, exterior, paint correction |
    | **Something else** | Any other service type |

    You must select at least one service to continue. You can add or remove services at any time after onboarding from **Settings → Services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your shop's size">
    Choose the option that best describes the current size of your team:

    - Just me
    - 2-3
    - 4-6
    - 7-12
    - 13\+

    ShopWrk uses this to set scheduling lanes and team defaults. After onboarding, invite people from **Team → Staff**. Email, name, and phone are required. Phone becomes their **Work Phone** for this shop. Team also has Schedules, Timesheet, Access, and Payroll Prep.

    Click **Finish setup** to complete the wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the wizard: showcase and theme

Once you finish the five wizard steps, ShopWrk takes you through two short post-wizard screens before opening the dashboard.

### Feature showcase

A 6-slide tour highlights what ShopWrk can do: responding to inbound leads, tier-based quoting, bay scheduling, inventory tracking, and self-closing estimates. Use the **Next capability** and **Back** buttons to move between slides.

If you'd rather skip straight to setup, click **Skip tour** in the top-right corner. You can revisit these features from the dashboard at any time.

### Theme selection

Choose your preferred display theme: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**, which follows your device's setting. You can change this later in your account settings.

## AI setup with Shopwrker

After the theme step, ShopWrk introduces you to Shopwrker, your built-in AI assistant. A short onboarding chat helps configure your pricing tiers, estimate templates, and customer portal in a few minutes of conversation.

<Tip>
  Take a few minutes for the Shopwrker chat now. It sets up your service pricing and templates automatically, which you'd otherwise configure manually in Settings.
</Tip>

If you'd prefer to skip this for now, click **skip for now** on the intro screen. You can open Shopwrker any time from the **AI** section in your dashboard navigation.

## Migration from another platform

If you're moving to ShopWrk from a platform like Urable, Tintwiz, Orbisx, GoHighLevel, or Square, look for the **Migration** step in your onboarding flow. It guides you through importing your existing data.

<Warning>
  Data migration is a one-time import. Review the migration guide for your platform before starting to avoid duplicate records.
</Warning>
