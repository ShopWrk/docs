After creating your account, ShopWrk guides you through a short setup wizard before taking you to your dashboard. The wizard collects your business name, location, services, and team size. After the wizard you'll see a feature tour, pick a theme, and meet your AI assistant, Shopwrker.

ShopWrk saves your data each time you complete a step. Clicking **Save & exit** skips the rest of onboarding and opens the dashboard without saving unfinished fields on the current step. Use **Resume setup** in the dashboard to return to where you left off.

## The setup wizard

The wizard has five steps. Each one takes under a minute to complete.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome — confirm your shop name">
    The first step asks you to confirm or set your shop's name.

    You can type the name directly, or use the **Find your business on Google** search to look up your shop. If ShopWrk finds a matching Google Business Profile, it prefills your name, address, phone number, and business hours automatically — saving you work in the steps that follow.

    <Tip>
      Searching for your business on Google at this step is optional, but it can cut the rest of the wizard down to a quick review rather than manual data entry.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address — enter your shop's location">
    Enter your shop's street address, city, state or province, and postal code. ShopWrk uses this to configure your booking page, invoices, and local service settings.

    If the welcome step pulled your address from a Google Business Profile, this step is skipped automatically and you're taken directly to the Confirm step to review what was imported.

    <Note>
      ShopWrk supports US and Canadian addresses. Select the correct country from the dropdown so the postal code format validates correctly.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm — review your shop details">
    This step shows a summary of what ShopWrk has collected so far: your shop name, address, phone number, website, and business hours.

    Review each field and edit anything that looks wrong. If you imported data from Google, this is the place to correct any details that didn't come through accurately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Services — choose what you offer">
    Select all the services your shop provides. ShopWrk uses this to configure your service catalog and set up relevant defaults.

    Available service types:

    | Service | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Window Tint** | Films, ceramic tints |
    | **Paint Protection Film** | Clear bras, full PPF |
    | **Ceramic Coating** | 9H coatings, graphene |
    | **Vinyl Wrap** | Color change, partial wraps |
    | **Detailing** | Interior, exterior, paint correction |
    | **Something else** | Any other service type |

    You must select at least one service to continue. You can add or remove services at any time after onboarding from **Settings → Services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team — select your shop's size">
    Choose the option that best describes the current size of your team:

    - Just me
    - 2–3
    - 4–6
    - 7–12
    - 13\+

    ShopWrk saves this answer to tailor setup guidance. You can invite team members after onboarding from the **Team** section of your dashboard, and set up bays in calendar settings.

    Click **Finish setup** to complete the wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the wizard: showcase, theme, migration, and AI setup

Once you finish the five wizard steps, ShopWrk takes you through the feature showcase, theme selection, Migration, and Shopwrker setup before opening the launch guide.

### Feature showcase

A 6-slide tour highlights what ShopWrk can do: responding to inbound leads, tier-based quoting, bay scheduling, inventory tracking, and self-closing estimates. Use the **Next capability** and **Back** buttons to move between slides.

Click **Skip tour** to leave onboarding and open the dashboard. This also skips the remaining theme, migration, and AI setup stages. You can return with the dashboard's **Resume setup** prompt, but the feature tour itself isn't available from the dashboard.

### Theme selection

Choose your preferred display theme: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (which follows your device's setting). You can change this later in your account settings.

## Migration from another platform

If you're moving to ShopWrk from another platform, look for the **Migration** step after the theme screen and before the Shopwrker chat. Urable and Tint Wiz import by CSV upload, GoHighLevel connects through an OAuth import, and **Something else** opens a concierge migration route. Orbisx and Square / Stripe appear in the step but are marked **Coming soon**.

<Warning>
  Data migration is a one-time import. Review the migration guide for your platform before starting to avoid duplicate records.
</Warning>

## AI setup with Shopwrker

After migration, ShopWrk introduces you to Shopwrker, your built-in AI assistant. The onboarding chat helps collect your pricing preferences, deposit policy, shop hours, days off, lead technician, service catalog, and review-message tone. It doesn't configure estimate templates or complete launch tasks like payments, messaging readiness, booking, or branding; those live in the Launch Guide.

<Tip>
  Take a few minutes for the Shopwrker chat now. It sets up your pricing and services automatically, which you'd otherwise configure manually in Settings.
</Tip>

If you'd rather move on, click **Skip, finish later**. ShopWrk marks onboarding as skipped and takes you to **Settings → Setup**, where you can continue the checklist. You can open Shopwrker any time from the **AI** section of the dashboard.

## The launch guide

The wizard gets you into the dashboard; the launch guide gets you live. It lives on your dashboard as a setup checklist and tracks everything a shop needs before taking real customers:

- Business profile complete
- Imported data reviewed (if you migrated)
- Services and tier pricing configured
- Calendar and online booking set up
- Payments verified end to end
- Messaging registered and sending
- Branding applied to documents
- AI agents configured or intentionally skipped
