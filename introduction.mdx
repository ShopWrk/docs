## What ShopWrk does

ShopWrk is built for shops that install window tint, paint protection film (PPF), ceramic coatings, vinyl wraps, and related services. Booking, estimates, jobs, invoices, and messages live in one dashboard your whole team can use.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Estimates and jobs" icon="file-invoice">
    Build tiered quotes, get customer approval, then schedule and track work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invoices and payments" icon="credit-card">
    Accept online payments with ShopWrk Pay, linked to the job invoice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CRM and pipeline" icon="address-book">
    Track leads, customers, and vehicles with full history and deal pipelines.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team scheduling" icon="calendar-days">
    Manage staff schedules, time tracking, and payroll in one place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Communications" icon="comments">
    SMS, voice, and email with your customers, in the same dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI assistant" icon="robot">
    An assistant that can help with jobs, estimates, invoices, and the rest of the shop.
  </Card>

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at ShopWrk and create your shop profile. See [Account setup](/getting-started/account-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    The guided onboarding wizard helps you add your services, set your pricing tiers, and invite your team. See [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Turn on ShopWrk Pay for customer payments, then connect Google Calendar and SMS. See [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first estimate">
    Create a customer, build a tier-based quote, and send it for approval. See [Estimates](/workflows/estimates).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your shop's data stays in your shop. Other shops on ShopWrk cannot see it.
</Note>
