## What ShopWrk does

ShopWrk is built specifically for shops that install window tint, paint protection film (PPF), ceramic coatings, vinyl wraps, and related services. It replaces scattered spreadsheets, generic CRMs, and disconnected payment tools with a single platform your whole team can use.

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at ShopWrk and create your shop profile. See [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    The guided onboarding wizard helps you add your services, set your pricing tiers, and invite your team. See [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Turn on ShopWrk Pay for customer payments, then connect Google Calendar and SMS. See [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first estimate">
    Create a customer, build a tier-based quote, and send it for approval. See [Estimates](/workflows/estimates).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ShopWrk is a multi-tenant platform — your shop's data is completely separate from other shops on the platform. All data is scoped to your shop account.
</Note>
