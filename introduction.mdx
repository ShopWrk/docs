## What ShopWrk does

Most shops run scheduling in one tool, messaging on personal phones, quotes in another app, and payment links in a third. Nothing talks to anything else, and leads go cold while someone finds the price sheet. ShopWrk replaces that pile with one system where every part feeds the next. The quote becomes the job, the job becomes the invoice, the invoice collects payment, and the finished job triggers the review request.

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up, verify your email if you used email signup, and enter the onboarding wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    The wizard collects your shop name, address, services, and team size. After it, the launch guide walks you through calendar, messaging registration, payments, and branding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Invite installers and staff, and set what each role can see and do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first estimate">
    Build a quote from your service catalog and send it by text or email for approval and deposit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each step has its own guide:

- [Account setup](/getting-started/account-setup)
- [Onboarding wizard](/getting-started/onboarding)
- [Roles and permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions)
- [Estimates](/workflows/estimates)

<Note>
  ShopWrk is a multi-tenant platform. Your shop's data is completely separate from other shops on the platform, and everything is scoped to your shop account.
</Note>
