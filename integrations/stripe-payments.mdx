Your ShopWrk subscription is billed through Stripe. This page covers how that works: where to update your card, where your receipts live, and what happens if a payment fails.

<Info>
  This is about paying for ShopWrk itself. Money your customers pay you runs through [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay) instead, which is a separate account with its own payouts.
</Info>

## What Stripe handles

- Your monthly or annual [plan subscription](/settings/billing)
- Plan upgrades, downgrades, and proration when you change tiers
- Overage charges for AI and messaging usage beyond your plan allowance
- A2P registration fees for SMS

## Managing your payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Go to **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage in Stripe">
    The Stripe customer portal opens in a new tab. You may need to confirm by email depending on your settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update what you need">
    From the portal you can change your card and download past invoices as PDFs. For plan changes and cancellation, come back to **Settings → Billing** in ShopWrk; those controls live in the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only users with the **Owner** role or billing permission can open the customer portal. Admins see the billing page but the button is disabled.

## Failed payments and past due

If a charge fails, Stripe retries it automatically and ShopWrk shows a past-due banner in your dashboard right away.

Paid features stay active for **7 days** past the due date. Update your payment method in the Stripe customer portal during that window and nothing else happens. After 7 days without a successful charge, paid features lock until the balance clears.

<Warning>
  Update your card as soon as you see the past-due banner. Seven days goes fast over a weekend, and re-locking an unlocked shop requires support intervention.
</Warning>

## Canceling

Cancel from **Settings → Billing** in ShopWrk. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and your shop keeps full access until that date.

Before canceling, export any records you want to keep. If you have questions about accessing a canceled shop later, contact ShopWrk support.
