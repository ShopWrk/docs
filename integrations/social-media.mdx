Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and Facebook Lead Ads are two separate connections in ShopWrk. You start both from **Integrations**. Do not start from Conversations. Do not start from Forms.

One connection is for DMs. The other is for lead forms. Connecting one does not turn the other on, and disconnecting one does not turn the other off.

## Two connections on Integrations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Instagram and Messenger" icon="instagram">
    Facebook Login for DMs. You pick which Facebook Pages this is for. Pages with a linked Instagram Business account get Instagram and Messenger. Pages without it connect as Messenger only, and the picker says so.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lead Ads" icon="facebook">
    A second Facebook Login for Facebook Lead Ads. You pick Pages again. After that, the Forms wizard still picks a Facebook lead form and maps its fields.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can connect the same Page for both. ShopWrk keeps them independent. A DM connection does not grant lead forms. A Lead Ads connection does not grant Instagram or Messenger.

## Connect Instagram and Messenger

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations**. Use the Instagram and Messenger card, not Conversations and not Forms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Facebook Login asks only for messaging permission. If messaging permission is missing, ShopWrk asks you to reconnect. It will not hide that behind a generic error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Pages">
    Choose which Facebook Pages this connection applies to. ShopWrk does not silently attach every Page on the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm what connected">
    A Page with a linked Instagram Business account shows Instagram and Messenger. A Page without one is labeled Messenger only. Instagram does not appear unless that linked account exists.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ShopWrk brings in a short stretch of recent DMs so Conversations is not empty after you connect. If that import fails, the connection still succeeds.

After you connect, you can turn extra Pages on or off for messaging from **Settings** without another Facebook Login. That lasts until the card shows **Needs reconnect**.

## Connect Facebook Lead Ads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations again">
    Use the **Lead Ads** card. It is the second Meta entry, not the Instagram and Messenger card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Facebook Login asks for Lead Ads permission only. It does not turn on ads tools inside ShopWrk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Pages">
    Choose which Pages should receive Facebook lead forms. Same picker idea as messaging. You are not connecting every Page by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the form in Forms">
    After the Page is connected, the existing Forms Facebook wizard still picks a form and maps fields. Form choice does not live inside Automations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A [Meta Lead Form automation](/features/automations#meta-lead-form) needs exactly one imported form. Connect Lead Ads here first, then import the form in Forms, then pick that form on the automation.
</Note>

## Needs reconnect

ShopWrk keeps the connection fresh on its own. When Facebook will not refresh it, the account shows **Needs reconnect** on Integrations.

Until you reconnect, ShopWrk will not send Instagram or Messenger replies on that connection, and it will not take in new Facebook leads for that connection. Click reconnect and complete Facebook Login again.

## Reply in Conversations

Once messaging is connected, Instagram and Messenger threads show up in **Conversations**.

### Images and files

You can send **1 to 10 images** in one Instagram or Messenger message. Eleven images are blocked before they leave ShopWrk.

You can send one video, one audio clip, or one file in a message. Do not mix a video with extra images. That send is rejected.

### How long you can reply

Meta limits how long a shop can message someone after they last wrote in.

- For **24 hours** after their last message, you can reply normally from Conversations.
- From **24 hours to 7 days**, you and your [automations](/features/automations) can still send. After 7 days, ShopWrk will not send.
- AI replies only work in the first 24 hours. If that window is closed, ShopWrk skips the send and shows why.

### Shared posts and stories

When a customer shares an Instagram post or reel in a DM, ShopWrk shows a preview image if Facebook provided one, plus a title. Click it to open the post on Instagram. If there is no image, you get a simple link, not an empty card.

When they reply to your story, the thread shows small text in the middle: **Replied to your story**. When they mention the shop in a story, it shows **Mentioned you in a story**. If they also typed words, those sit in a normal bubble. ShopWrk does not play the story in the thread.

## Disconnect one connection without losing the other

On Integrations, **Disconnect** on Instagram and Messenger turns DMs off for those Pages. Lead Ads on the same Page stay on.

**Disconnect** on Lead Ads turns lead forms off. Instagram and Messenger on that Page stay on.

Neither disconnect removes the other connection's Pages.

## Automations that use these connections

After messaging is connected, automations can wait for an Instagram DM, a Facebook message, an Instagram comment, or a Facebook comment. See [Automations](/features/automations#message-and-comment-triggers).

After Lead Ads is connected and a form is imported, use **Meta Lead Form** with exactly one form. See [Meta Lead Form](/features/automations#meta-lead-form).
