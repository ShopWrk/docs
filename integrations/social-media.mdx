ShopWrk's social media tools let you manage your shop's online presence from one place. Connect your accounts, compose posts with AI-assisted captions and hashtags, schedule them in advance, and review performance metrics — all without switching between apps.

## Connect your social accounts

Go to **Settings → Social Accounts** to link your platforms.

### Connecting Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

Meta's Instagram Business publishing requires a connected Facebook Page. ShopWrk handles this through a single OAuth connection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Connect' on the Meta card">
    You'll be redirected to Facebook to log in and choose which Page to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant all requested permissions">
    ShopWrk needs permission to manage your Page posts and, if applicable, your linked Instagram Business account. Decline any permission and that platform won't be available for publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm both accounts appear">
    After completing OAuth, ShopWrk shows separate entries for your Facebook Page and Instagram Business account, each with a **Connected** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Instagram publishing through the Meta API requires an Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Personal Instagram accounts are not supported.
</Note>

### Connecting TikTok and Google Business

For TikTok and Google Business, click **Connect** next to the platform. You'll go through that platform's OAuth flow and return to ShopWrk once authorized.

### Account statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Connected | Active and ready to publish |
| Needs Reconnect | Authorization expired — click Reconnect |
| Expired | Token has lapsed — reconnect to restore access |

If an account shows **Needs Reconnect** or **Expired**, posts scheduled to that account will not publish until you reconnect.

## Creating and scheduling posts

Go to **Marketing → Social Planner** and click **New Post**, or navigate to **Marketing → Social → Create Post**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your caption">
    Type your post copy in the caption field. ShopWrk shows a live platform preview so you can see how the post will look on each channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Upload images or video. Each platform has its own format requirements — ShopWrk warns you if an uploaded file doesn't meet the target platform's specs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select target accounts">
    Choose one or more connected accounts to publish to. You can post the same content to multiple platforms at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or publish">
    - **Publish now** — sends the post immediately to all selected accounts.
    - **Schedule** — pick a date and time. The post appears in the Social Planner calendar and publishes automatically at the chosen time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### AI caption and hashtag tools

The post composer includes AI-powered tools to help you write content faster:

- **Generate caption** — describe your post (for example, "front window tint on a black Tesla Model 3") and the AI drafts a ready-to-publish caption.
- **Suggest hashtags** — generates relevant hashtags based on your caption and auto-styling niche.
- **Generate image** — creates a social media graphic using AI if you don't have a photo to attach.

<Tip>
  The AI strategist is trained on auto-styling content. Giving it specific details — film type, vehicle make, finish result — produces more useful output than a generic prompt.
</Tip>

### Draft saving

Posts are auto-saved as drafts while you compose them. If you leave the page before scheduling or publishing, your draft is waiting in the Social Planner when you return.

## Social Planner calendar

The Social Planner at **Marketing → Social Planner** shows all your scheduled and published posts in a calendar view. Click any post to edit it, reschedule it, or view its performance after publishing.

## Analytics

Go to **Marketing → Social → Analytics** to view aggregated engagement data across your connected accounts. The default view covers the last 30 days and shows metrics by platform.

Metrics available include impressions, reach, engagement, and post-level performance. Use this view to identify which content types and posting times resonate with your audience.

<Note>
  Analytics data is pulled from each platform's API and may be delayed by up to 24 hours depending on the platform.
</Note>

## Disconnecting an account

In **Settings → Social Accounts**, click **Disconnect** next to any account. Existing published posts are not affected. Scheduled posts targeting only that account will not publish after disconnection.
