ShopWrk texts and calling use one shop phone number. Customers text and call that number. You answer in Chrome or on a Work Phone. Every thread lives in Conversations.

## SMS setup

### First-time setup

If you want customer texts to actually send, go to **Settings → Messaging** and finish the registration steps ShopWrk shows there.

US carriers require this once for business texts. Until it is done, outbound texts can get filtered or blocked.

ShopWrk then lets you pick a shop number and set messaging defaults on that same screen. Do the registration first.

### Finish registration

ShopWrk asks for the shop details carriers need. Typical items:

- Legal name, address, tax ID (EIN), website, and a contact
- Owner name, email, and phone
- A short note on how you will use texts, such as appointment reminders, follow-ups, or promotions

Submit it, then wait. Review is often 1 to 5 business days. Watch the status on **Settings → Messaging**. Both parts need to show approved before ShopWrk can send. You can still receive texts while you wait.

<Warning>
  Do not send a pile of texts before approval. Carriers can flag the number for good, which means a new number and another registration.
</Warning>

### Get a phone number

Get your shop number from **Settings → Messaging**. Pick a local area code that matches your market. Customers pick up more often when the number looks local.

If you already have a business number you want to keep, contact ShopWrk support about porting it in.

### Compliance and trust

**Settings → Messaging** includes a **Compliance & Trust** panel where you can monitor:

- Brand registration status
- Campaign approval status
- Monthly SMS usage, inbound and outbound counts, and estimated cost
- Opt-out compliance status

#### SMS opt-out

ShopWrk handles STOP, HELP, and UNSTOP keywords. When a customer replies STOP, they are added to your opt-out list and will not get further texts from your number. ShopWrk blocks sends to opted-out numbers.

You can view and manage the opt-out list from the Compliance & Trust panel.

## Messaging configuration

Once your number is in place and registration is approved, **Settings → Messaging** has saved **Message templates** for jobs like appointment confirmations, invoice reminders, and review requests. You can turn on **Auto-replies** for after-hours or missed messages, and manage the **Opt-out list**.

## Bulk text campaigns

Use bulk texts when you need to reach many customers at once, for promotions, seasonal offers, or service reminders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your audience">
    Filter your customer list by things like service type, last visit date, or vehicle type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Use a saved template or write a custom message. Keep it short. Carrier filtering gets worse past 160 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Preview the recipient count and the message, then send. ShopWrk queues the texts and sends them from your shop number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Bulk texts should match what you said you would send when you registered. Offers outside that can get filtered.
</Warning>

## Call Flow

Go to **Settings → Calling**. Owners and admins edit Call Flow.

You will see your shop number, then **Primary recipient**. Pick the teammate who should get the first ring. Add their **Work phone** on the same page, or they will show as **Work phone needed**. Choose fallbacks if you want them, then save.

If Call Flow is missing or turned off, inbound calls go to ShopWrk voicemail. That is better than a silent miss.

### Team Work Phone

A Work Phone belongs to one teammate in this shop. It is the cell that rings with their Chrome tabs.

Set it in either place:

- **Team**, open that person, then **Work phone**.
- **Settings → Calling**, on the person you picked for Call Flow.

Call Flow cannot use a teammate as a phone target until they have a Work Phone. After you save a number, click **Test phone**. You should hear: "Your ShopWrk Work Phone is connected. Live customer calls can ring this phone."

### Primary recipient and Chrome

When a customer dials your shop number, ShopWrk rings every ready Chrome tab for that person and their Work Phone at the same time, for 18 seconds. A closed laptop does not delay the cell phone.

Keep ShopWrk open in Chrome so that person can answer in the browser.

### Press 1 on a Work Phone

When a Work Phone rings, you hear: "ShopWrk business call. Press 1 to accept."

Press 1. If you only swipe to answer and skip press 1, the call can land in that person's personal voicemail instead of ShopWrk voicemail.

### Fallback

If the Primary Recipient does not answer, ShopWrk rings up to three fallback teammates and an optional fallback number together for 12 seconds. Ready Chrome tabs and Work Phones ring at the same time in that stage too.

The first person who answers in Chrome or presses 1 on a phone wins. Everyone else stops ringing.

If the Primary Recipient is already on a ShopWrk call, ShopWrk skips them and goes to fallback so the next customer is not stuck waiting.

If nobody accepts, the caller reaches ShopWrk voicemail and the call shows as missed.

### After hours

Call Flow uses the shop's business hours and timezone. Those same hours are used by scheduling and online booking.

When the shop is closed you can pick:

- **Normal call flow**: keep primary and fallback routing.
- **Fallback only**: skip the Primary Recipient.
- **Voicemail**: send callers straight to ShopWrk voicemail.

## Incoming calls in Chrome

Keep ShopWrk open in Chrome with calling ready. When a customer calls, the incoming-call screen shows on every ready Chrome tab, not just one of them.

Answer in the browser, or press 1 on the Work Phone. The first accepted destination wins.

The call also opens a CALL thread in Conversations, even if nobody picks up.

## Outbound calls

Start a call from a customer thread in Conversations, or from the customer's profile. As soon as dialing begins, Conversations creates a CALL thread. You do not wait for hangup, and you do not need recording turned on.

A saved customer attaches to that thread. An unknown number still gets a visible CALL thread.

While the call is live, Conversations shows Calling, Ringing, or On a call.

## Call recording

Recording is a shop toggle, not something you start on each call.

Go to **Settings → Calling**. If you see tabs, open **Setup**. Turn on **Call recording enabled**, then save.

When it is on, ShopWrk records after a person answers, in Chrome or after press 1 on a Work Phone. Open the CALL thread in Conversations to play the audio. ShopWrk then writes a transcript and a summary on that same thread. If the transcript or summary fails, you still keep the audio and the call history.

When it is off, the CALL thread still appears in Conversations. There is no audio, transcript, or summary.

Ringing, no-answer attempts, and a personal carrier voicemail are not recorded as completed conversations.

<Warning>
  Recording rules differ by state. ShopWrk plays a disclosure when recording is on. Know the consent rules where you operate.
</Warning>
