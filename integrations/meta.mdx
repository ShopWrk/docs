Half the leads a styling shop gets start on Instagram or Facebook. Connecting your Meta accounts brings those conversations and leads into ShopWrk instead of leaving them stranded in an app on your personal phone.

Go to **Settings → Integrations** and connect Facebook to begin. Instagram connects through the same flow.

## What connecting gives you

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="DMs in your inbox" icon="inbox">
    Instagram and Messenger threads appear alongside SMS in Conversations, with full history. Your whole team sees them, not just whoever has the password.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lead forms" icon="filter">
    Meta Lead Ad submissions create contacts and trigger automations the moment they come in.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lead closing over DM" icon="user-plus">
    With the lead closer enabled, DMs get answered in seconds and worked toward a quote and booking.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ad attribution" icon="chart-line">
    On Autopilot, ad spend connects to the leads, bookings, and revenue each campaign produced.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connecting step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Under **Settings → Integrations**, click **Connect** on Facebook. A Meta login window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and authorize">
    Use the Facebook account that administers your business page and Instagram account. ShopWrk requests only the permissions needed for messaging and lead capture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your pages">
    Pick which Facebook pages and which Instagram business account ShopWrk should listen to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    New DMs start arriving in Conversations within minutes. Existing message history imports where Meta provides it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping the connection healthy

Meta connections expire occasionally, usually after a password change or a permissions review. When that happens, ShopWrk flags the account under **Settings → Integrations** with a **Needs reconnect** badge, and messaging pauses on that channel until you click reconnect. Nothing is lost; threads resume where they left off.

<Warning>
  If your shop runs its Instagram through a marketing agency's login, connect the account you own instead. Connections tied to an agency's credentials break whenever their relationship with your page changes.
</Warning>

## Who can reply

DMs land in the shared inbox, so any teammate with Conversations access can answer. With the lead closer configured for social channels, drafts or automatic replies follow the same rules you set for SMS. See [AI agents](/features/ai-agents) for routing options per channel.
