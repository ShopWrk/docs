Half the leads a styling shop gets start on Instagram or Facebook. Connecting your Meta accounts brings those conversations and leads into ShopWrk instead of leaving them stranded in an app on your personal phone.

Go to **Settings → Integrations**. Meta has two separate connections, and each one is worth connecting:

- **Meta Messaging** brings Facebook Messenger and Instagram DMs into Conversations.
- **Meta Lead Ads** delivers lead-form submissions straight into your pipeline.

You can enable either connection per Facebook Page you manage, and they connect independently.

## Connecting step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Under **Settings → Integrations**, click **Connect** on the Meta Messaging card (or Meta Lead Ads for lead forms). A Meta login window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and authorize">
    Use the Facebook account that administers your business page and Instagram account. ShopWrk requests only the permissions needed for messaging and lead capture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your pages">
    Pick which Facebook pages, and which Instagram business account, the connection should serve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it works">
    For messaging, send a test DM to your page; new conversations should arrive in Conversations within minutes. Some recent history may appear, but don't rely on ShopWrk importing the page's full past inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping the connection healthy

A Meta connection can need attention after a password change or permissions review. **Settings → Integrations** shows a **Needs reconnect** badge on the affected connection, and that function pauses until you reconnect. Messaging and Lead Ads reconnect separately from their own cards; refreshing one doesn't disturb the other. Nothing is lost; threads resume where they left off.

<Warning>
  If your shop runs its Instagram through a marketing agency's login, connect the account you own instead. Connections tied to an agency's credentials break whenever their relationship with your page changes.
</Warning>

## Who can reply

DMs land in the shared inbox, so any teammate with Conversations access can answer. With the lead closer configured for social channels, drafts or automatic replies follow the same rules you set for SMS. See [AI agents](/features/ai-agents) for routing options per channel.
