Sign in as a technician and the app gives you your workday: a clock card, today's assigned jobs, your running hours, and your messages. The bottom bar opens Schedule, Create, Inbox, and ShopWrker. Two of those are still placeholders, and this page says exactly which.

## Home screen

Pull down anywhere to refresh.

### Greeting

A time-sensitive greeting sits at the top of the screen: "Good morning," "Good afternoon," or "Good evening."

## Clocking in and out

The clock card is the first interactive element on your home screen.

**Off the clock**, it shows a neutral status with a prompt to start your shift. **On the clock**, it turns green with a running timer that updates while the app is open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clock in">
    Tap **Clock in**. The button flips to **Clock out**, the card turns green, and a haptic confirms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work your shift">
    The elapsed timer keeps counting even if you leave the app. Your shift records on the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock out">
    Tap **Clock out** when you're done. A warning haptic confirms and your hours save automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the card says you're clocked in at another shop, switch back to that shop from the account menu before clocking out.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Clock corrections happen in the web dashboard by an owner or manager. Get mistakes fixed the same day so payroll stays clean.
</Warning>

## My jobs today

The **My jobs today** card lists every job assigned to you today: scheduled time, customer, vehicle, and status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting | Created but not yet scheduled |
| Scheduled | Upcoming, not started |
| In Progress | Actively being worked |
| Pending Review | Finished, awaiting owner approval |
| Completed | Done |
| Cancelled | Not happening |
| Rescheduled | Moved to a new date |

No assignments today? The card says so.

<Tip>
  Assignments come from your owner in the web dashboard. Missing a job you expected? Ask them.
</Tip>

## Time tracking

The Time card shows two numbers:

| Row | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Hours and minutes clocked so far today |
| **This week** | Total since the start of the week |

A progress bar fills as you approach a standard 40-hour week.

## Messages preview

The messages card at the bottom of home previews your most recent conversations: sender, snippet, and how long ago. An unread count badge appears on the header when something is waiting.

## Inbox

The full Inbox tab works today. Search and filter conversations, open a thread, start a conversation, and reply where the channel allows it.

## Create

The Create tab previews five shortcuts (new job, estimate, invoice, customer, vehicle). All five currently show a **Coming soon** notice; creation happens on the web dashboard for now.

## Schedule

Your job calendar is coming soon. Check **My jobs today** on Home for your assignments, or use the web dashboard for the full calendar.

## ShopWrker

The ShopWrker tab opens the AI assistant for questions about your jobs, customers, or operations. From inside, open **Artifacts** for structured outputs like summaries and reports.

<Tip>
  "What are my jobs today?" or "What's the status of the Johnson job?" gets better answers than vague questions.
</Tip>

## Notifications

Push notifications can reach you while the app is closed. Tapping an inbox-message or AI-response notification opens its related screen. Other notification types open the app without a guaranteed direct link.

Manage delivery in your iPhone's **Settings → Notifications → ShopWrk**.
