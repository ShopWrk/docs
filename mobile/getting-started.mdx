Getting the ShopWrk iOS app on your iPhone takes a few minutes. You sign in with the same account you use on the web, and the app opens the right experience for your role automatically.

## Before you begin

<Info>
  You need an active ShopWrk account to sign in. If you don't have one yet, ask your shop owner or visit [shopwrk.com](https://shopwrk.com).
</Info>

Make sure you have:

- An iPhone running iOS 16 or later
- Your ShopWrk email and password, or access to Apple ID or Google Sign-In

## Install the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App Store">
    Tap the App Store icon on your home screen or search for it in Spotlight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for ShopWrk">
    Type **ShopWrk** in the search bar. Look for the official ShopWrk app by ShopWrk, Inc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Get and install">
    Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password and wait for the download to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Tap **Open** in the App Store, or find the ShopWrk icon on your home screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your shop is running a pre-release version of ShopWrk, you may be invited to install through Apple TestFlight instead. Open the TestFlight link your shop owner sends you and tap **Install**.
</Tip>

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your sign-in method">
    Pick one:

    - **Email and password**, for accounts set up with email
    - **Continue with Apple**
    - **Continue with Google**, if your ShopWrk account is linked to Google
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    For email sign-in, type your email, then your password, then tap **Sign in**. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** to reset it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the system prompt">
    For Apple or Google sign-in, follow the prompt. Face ID or Touch ID confirms automatically where enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If sign-in fails, check that you're using the same email tied to your ShopWrk account. Ask your shop owner if you're unsure which one that is.
</Warning>

## Which experience you get

The app reads your role at sign-in and routes you accordingly. Owner and Admin accounts land on the owner experience with live KPIs, today's schedule preview, quick-action tiles, recent activity, the inbox, contacts, search, and ShopWrker. Technician accounts land on the technician experience with clock-in/out, today's assigned jobs, hours worked, and a message preview.

Full walkthroughs: [Owner features](/mobile/owner-features) and [Technician features](/mobile/technician-features).

## The bottom tab bar

Both experiences share the same five tabs.

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your role's dashboard: KPIs for owners, shift tools for technicians |
| **Schedule** | Job calendar view, coming soon; use the web dashboard meanwhile |
| **Create** | Quick-start sheet for jobs, estimates, invoices, customers, and vehicles; forms coming soon |
| **Inbox** | Customer conversations: find, read, start, and reply |
| **ShopWrker** | AI assistant for shop questions, plus saved artifacts |

A search bar sits at the top of Home for finding customers, vehicles, jobs, invoices, and estimates.
