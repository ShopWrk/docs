The ShopWrk iOS app is the mobile companion to your ShopWrk web dashboard. It puts the most important shop information in your pocket — live KPIs for owners, time tracking and job lists for technicians — without requiring you to open a browser. The app is designed for iPhone and optimized for how auto styling professionals actually work: quickly, on the floor, between jobs.

## Who it's for

ShopWrk has two distinct mobile experiences that activate based on the role tied to your account.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shop owners" icon="building-2" href="/mobile/owner-features">
    See live revenue, job progress, and pipeline metrics from your home screen. Access contacts, CRM, payments, and inventory from quick-action tiles, and use the AI assistant to get answers about your shop.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Technicians" icon="wrench" href="/mobile/technician-features">
    Clock in and out of your shift, view the jobs assigned to you today, track your hours for the day and week, and preview incoming messages — all from a single home screen.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## iOS-first design

The app is built for iPhone. It uses native iOS tab navigation, native system fonts, and iOS-native interaction patterns such as haptic feedback on primary actions and pull-to-refresh on every scrollable screen.

<Note>
  The ShopWrk iOS app is currently available for iPhone only. An Android version is not available at this time.
</Note>

## What you can do in the app vs. the web dashboard

The mobile app is focused on the tasks you need to do quickly and on the go. For more involved workflows — building estimates, editing invoices, configuring shop settings, or managing team permissions — use the [ShopWrk web dashboard](https://app.shopwrk.com).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile app">
    - View live dashboard KPIs (revenue, jobs, pipeline, AI activity)
    - See today's schedule at a glance
    - Clock in and out of a shift
    - View assigned jobs and their statuses
    - Track hours worked today and this week
    - Preview inbox messages
    - Quick-create a job, estimate, invoice, customer, or vehicle
    - Search across customers, vehicles, jobs, invoices, and estimates
    - Chat with the built-in AI assistant
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web dashboard">
    - Build and send estimates and invoices
    - Manage job details, services, and line items
    - Configure scheduling and technician assignments
    - Manage customers, vehicles, and leads in depth
    - Set up team roles and permissions
    - Configure shop settings, integrations, and billing
    - View full historical reports and analytics
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Key sections in the app

Every screen in the app belongs to one of the following areas.

| Section | Owner | Technician | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Yes | Yes | Dashboard with KPIs or shift tools depending on role |
| Schedule | Yes | Yes | Job calendar view (coming soon — use the web app for now) |
| Create | Yes | Yes | Quick-start sheet for jobs, estimates, invoices, customers, and vehicles |
| Inbox | Yes | Yes | Unified conversation inbox (coming soon) |
| Assistant | Yes | Yes | AI assistant for shop-related questions |
| Search | Yes | Yes | Global search across all records |
| Contacts | Yes | — | Customer and lead list |
| CRM | Yes | — | Pipeline and deal stages |
| Payments | Yes | — | Invoices and payout tracking |
| Inventory | Yes | — | Stock and supplies management |

<Info>
  Sections marked "coming soon" are available as placeholders in the app today. Full functionality is being added in upcoming releases. Use the ShopWrk web dashboard in the meantime.
</Info>
