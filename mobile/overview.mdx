The ShopWrk iOS app is the mobile companion to the ShopWrk web dashboard. It covers the moments you are not at a desk: checking the day from the parts store, clocking in on the floor, replying to a customer between installs. Some deeper workflows still belong to the web app, and this page tells you exactly which is which.

## Who it's for

The app has two experiences based on your account's role. Owner and Admin accounts get the owner experience; Technician accounts get the technician experience.

## iPhone-first design

Built natively for iPhone: native tab navigation, ShopWrk's Satoshi typeface sized for thumb reach, haptic feedback on primary actions, and pull-to-refresh on key screens.

<Note>
  The ShopWrk iOS app is currently available for iPhone only. An Android version is not available at this time.
</Note>

## What works in the app today

- Live home-screen KPIs for owners: revenue, jobs, pipeline, AI activity
- Clock in and out with running hours for technicians
- Today's assigned jobs with statuses
- The Inbox: find, read, start, and reply to customer conversations across channels
- Contacts: browse, search, add, and open full contact records
- Global search across customers, vehicles, jobs, invoices, and estimates (results are view-only for now)
- The ShopWrker assistant for shop questions
- Push notifications for messages and AI responses

## What still lives on the web

- Building and sending estimates and invoices (the mobile Create sheet shows these as coming soon)
- The full calendar view (the mobile Schedule tab is coming soon)
- Shop settings, integrations, billing, team permissions
- CRM pipeline management, payment operations, and inventory management beyond quick checks

For any of that, use the [ShopWrk web dashboard](https://app.shopwrk.com).
