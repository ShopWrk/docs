Sign in as an owner or admin and the app gives you a live picture of the shop from anywhere. The home screen carries your metrics, today's schedule preview, quick actions, and recent activity. Search, Create, Inbox, and ShopWrker sit one tap away in the bottom bar.

## Home screen

Pull down anywhere on the home screen to refresh its data.

### Greeting and status line

A greeting changes with the time of day and includes your first name. Before 5 a.m. it says "Working late." Below it, an amber pill shows how many jobs are in the bay, and a second line shows how many are still scheduled today.

### KPI tiles

Four live tiles in a two-column grid, each with the last seven days charted:

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue · Today** | Collected today, with a 7-day sparkline |
| **Jobs · Today** | Done or in progress out of today's total |
| **Pipeline · Open** | Value of open estimates and pending approvals |
| **AI Agent · Today** | AI interactions today, with a 7-day bar chart |

### Today's schedule

Up to three of today's jobs appear as a card: scheduled time, customer, vehicle, and status color.

- **Amber:** in bay or under review
- **Green:** completed or scheduled
- **Red:** cancelled

An "Up next" chip shows when the following job is due. Tap **View full day** for the full Schedule tab, which is coming soon; use the web dashboard for full calendar management meanwhile.

### Activity feed

The bottom of the home screen lists shop events from the last 24 hours: jobs created, jobs completed, invoices sent, payments received. Each row has a detail line and a relative timestamp.

## Inbox

The Inbox tab is where customer conversations live on mobile. Search and filter threads, open any conversation, start a new one, and reply on supported channels including SMS and connected social accounts. The list opens on SMS by default; switch channels or view all from the channel menu.

## Contacts

Open Contacts from the quick-action tile. You can browse, search, and filter customers and leads, add a contact, and open any record to see its profile details, activity, vehicles, notes, tasks, jobs, estimates, invoices, payments, and deals.

## Search

Tap the search bar at the top of Home. Type at least two characters and results appear across customers, vehicles, jobs, invoices, and estimates. Results are view-only for now; open records through Contacts or the web dashboard.

## CRM, Payments, Inventory tiles

These quick-action destinations show placeholder views while their mobile experiences are built. Use the web dashboard for pipeline work, payment operations, and inventory management in the meantime.

## ShopWrker

The ShopWrker tab (sparkle icon) is your AI assistant. Ask about jobs, revenue, customers, or operations. From inside ShopWrker you can also open **Artifacts**, structured outputs like summaries and reports the assistant has produced.

<Tip>
  Specific questions beat broad ones. "How many jobs did we complete this week?" gets a sharper answer than "how's business?"
</Tip>

## Notifications

Push notifications can alert you while the app is in the background. Tapping an inbox-message or AI-response notification opens its related screen directly. Other notification types open the app without a guaranteed direct link.

Manage delivery in your iPhone's **Settings → Notifications → ShopWrk**.
