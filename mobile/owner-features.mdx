When you sign in as a shop owner, the ShopWrk iOS app gives you a real-time picture of your business from anywhere. Your home screen shows live metrics, today's job schedule, quick-access tiles for the most common workflows, and a feed of recent activity — all in one scrollable view. Additional tools like search, global create, and the AI assistant are always a tap away from the bottom tab bar.

## Home screen

The owner home screen is your operational command center. Pull down to refresh any section and get the latest data from your shop.

### Greeting and status line

At the top of the home screen you'll see a time-sensitive greeting — "Good morning," "Good afternoon," or "Good evening" — personalized with your first name. Below the greeting, an amber pill shows how many jobs are currently in the bay, and a secondary line shows how many jobs are scheduled for the rest of the day.

### KPI tiles

Four live metric tiles appear in a two-column grid immediately below the greeting. Each tile includes a mini chart that plots the last seven days of data.

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Revenue · Today** | Total revenue collected today, with a sparkline of the past 7 days |
| **Jobs · Today** | Jobs done or in progress today out of the total scheduled, with dot-progress indicators |
| **Pipeline · Open** | Total value of open estimates and pending approvals |
| **AI Agent · Today** | Number of AI assistant interactions today, with a 7-day bar chart |

### Today's schedule

Below the KPI tiles, a card shows up to three jobs on today's calendar. The card header displays the current time and highlights the job that's active right now alongside an "Up next" chip showing when the following job is due. Each row in the card shows the scheduled time, customer name, vehicle, and a color-coded status label.

Status colors at a glance:

- **Amber** — in bay or under review
- **Green** — completed or scheduled
- **Red** — cancelled or no-show

Tap **View full day** in the section header to open the full Schedule tab.

<Note>
  The Schedule tab is coming in a future release. For now, the "View full day" link is a placeholder. Use the ShopWrk web dashboard to manage your full calendar.
</Note>

### Activity feed

At the bottom of the home screen, a "Recent activity" section shows a chronological list of shop events from the last 24 hours: new jobs created, jobs completed, invoices sent, and payments received. Each row shows the event title, a detail line, and a relative timestamp (for example, "2 min ago").

## Create

The **Create** tab (the `+` in the bottom tab bar) opens a quick-start action sheet. It lets you kick off any of the five most common creation tasks without navigating away from what you're doing.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New job** | Opens a job for a customer and vehicle |
| **New estimate** | Builds an estimate to send for customer approval |
| **New invoice** | Creates an invoice to charge for completed work |
| **New customer** | Adds a new customer and their first vehicle |
| **New vehicle** | Attaches a vehicle to an existing customer |

<Info>
  Full create flows for estimates, invoices, customers, and vehicles are being added in an upcoming release. Tapping those rows currently shows a "Coming soon" notice. New job creation is available now.
</Info>

## Search

Tap the search icon in the top-right corner of the home screen to open the search overlay. The overlay auto-focuses a search field and shows a "Browse" list of categories while the field is empty.

Start typing to search across:

- Customers
- Vehicles
- Jobs
- Invoices
- Estimates

Results appear as you type. Tap any result to open the record.

## AI assistant

The **Assistant** tab (sparkle icon) opens a conversational AI interface trained on your shop's data. Ask it questions about your jobs, revenue, customers, or general shop operations.

You can also view **Artifacts** — structured outputs the assistant has generated, such as summaries and reports — by navigating to the artifacts screen from within the Assistant tab.

<Tip>
  The AI assistant works best with specific questions. For example: "How many jobs did we complete this week?" or "Which technician has the most open jobs today?" rather than open-ended prompts.
</Tip>

## Notifications

Push notifications keep you informed of key shop events even when the app is in the background. ShopWrk sends notifications for new job activity, payment events, and messages. Tap any notification to open the relevant screen directly.

To manage which notifications you receive, go to your iPhone's **Settings → Notifications → ShopWrk**.
