Apply your shop branding to every document and page customers see. Manage it from **Settings → Branding**, with tabs for Invoice, Estimate, Email, Booking Page, and Portal. Each tab saves on its own, so you can customize each surface separately.

<Note>
  Invoices and estimates share the same logo and accent color. Updating those fields on either tab updates both document types.
</Note>

## Invoice branding

The Invoice tab controls how invoices look when customers receive them or view them in the portal.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Accent color">
    Pick your brand color. Paste the color code from your logo (example `#14b8a6`), or use the color picker if the screen shows one. That color tints invoice headers and accents. The same color applies to estimates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Logo">
    Enter a publicly accessible URL for your logo. Recommended size is 200×50 px in PNG or JPG. The logo appears at the top of printed and digital invoices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invoice number prefix">
    Set a short alphanumeric prefix (up to 10 characters: letters, numbers, and hyphens) before the invoice number. For example, `INV-` produces `INV-0001`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Footer and terms">
    Add a footer message (up to 500 characters) at the bottom of every invoice. You can also set default terms (up to 2,000 characters) that prefill on new invoices and can be edited per invoice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Estimate branding

The Estimate tab shares logo and accent color with Invoice. It also has estimate-specific settings.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Estimate number prefix">
    Set a prefix before each estimate number. Default is `EST-`, producing numbers like `EST-0001`. Letters, numbers, and hyphens only, up to 10 characters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Footer and terms">
    Add a footer and default terms for estimates. They prefill on new estimates and can be overridden per estimate. Footer max 500 characters; terms max 2,000.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Default tier presets">
    When you create a tiered estimate (Good / Better / Best), ShopWrk prefills tier names and descriptions from these presets. Defaults are Good, Better, and Best. Rename them, add descriptions, or remove unused tiers. You can configure up to five presets.

    If no presets exist, click **Use Default (Good / Better / Best)** to add the standard three, then customize.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Email branding

The Email tab sets the header and footer customers see on shop email. That includes invoices, estimates, booking mail, and ShopWrk Pay payment messages.

Logo, colors, tagline, and footer live here. Who the mail is from is set on [Send email from your shop domain](/settings/sending-domain). Until that domain is verified, emails still send from a shared ShopWrk address. After it is verified, they send as your shop.

### Header and logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set an accent color">
    Pick the color for the header bar and call-to-action buttons. Paste a color code (example `#14b8a6`) or use the picker if shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your logo URL">
    Provide a publicly accessible logo URL. The logo appears centered in the email header. Recommended size is 200×50 px.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tagline">
    Enter a short tagline (up to 100 characters) under your logo. For example: *Professional Auto Styling Services*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Footer and social links

The email footer appears at the bottom of every outgoing email. You can add:

- A custom footer message (up to 500 characters)
- An Instagram profile URL
- A Facebook page URL
- Your shop website URL

Social links appear as icons in the footer.

## Booking page branding

Your booking page is where customers request appointments. Customize it on the Booking Page tab before you share the link.

The booking page URL is at the top of the tab. Copy it or click **Preview** to open it live.

### Hero section

Set a **hero title** and **hero subtitle** at the top of the booking page.

- **Hero title** example: *Premium Auto Styling*
- **Hero subtitle** example: *Window Tint, PPF, Ceramic Coating & More*

### Colors and images

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Accent color | Brand color for buttons and highlights on the booking page |
| Logo URL | Your shop logo on the booking page |
| Background image URL | Optional hero background behind the title and subtitle |

<Tip>
  Use a high-contrast background with a dark overlay so the hero title stays readable. A wide photo of your shop or finished vehicles works well.
</Tip>

## Saving your changes

Each branding tab has its own **Save Settings** button. Changes apply only after you save. If you leave with unsaved changes, ShopWrk asks whether to discard them.
