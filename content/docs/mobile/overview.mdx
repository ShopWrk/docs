The ShopWrk iOS app covers the moments you are not at a desk: checking the day from the parts store, clocking in on the floor, replying to a customer between installs. Deeper work still belongs on the web dashboard, and this page says which is which.

## Who it's for

The app shows different home screens based on your permissions. People with owner-style access get the owner home. Technicians get the technician home. Exact access still follows your [permission groups](/getting-started/roles-permissions).

## What you can do on iPhone today

- See today's revenue and jobs on Home (owners and managers)
- Clock in and out with running hours (technicians)
- Review today's assigned jobs and statuses
- Find, read, start, and reply to conversations in Inbox
- Browse, search, add, and open Contacts
- Search customers, vehicles, jobs, invoices, and estimates (view-only results for now)
- Ask ShopWrker shop questions
- Get push notifications for messages and assistant replies

Pull down on Home to refresh. iPhone only for now. Android is not available yet.

## What still lives on the web

- Building and sending estimates and invoices
- The full calendar (the mobile Schedule tab is not ready yet)
- Shop settings, integrations, billing, and team permissions
- CRM pipeline work, payment operations, and inventory management

For those, use the [ShopWrk web dashboard](https://app.shopwrk.com). See [CRM](/features/crm), [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay), and [Inventory](/features/inventory).
