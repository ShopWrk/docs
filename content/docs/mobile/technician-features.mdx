Sign in as a technician and Home shows your workday: clock card, today's assigned jobs, running hours, and message previews. Schedule and Create are still coming soon; this page says what works today.

## Home screen

Pull down anywhere to refresh.

## Clocking in and out

The clock card is at the top of Home.

**Off the clock**, it prompts you to start your shift. **On the clock**, it turns green with a running timer while the app is open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clock in">
    Tap **Clock in**. The button flips to **Clock out** and the card turns green.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work your shift">
    The timer keeps counting even if you leave the app. Your shift records on the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock out">
    Tap **Clock out** when you are done. Your hours save automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the card says you are clocked in at another shop, switch back to that shop from the account menu before clocking out.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Clock corrections happen in the web dashboard by someone with permission. Get mistakes fixed the same day so payroll stays clean.
</Warning>

## My jobs today

**My jobs today** lists every job assigned to you today: scheduled time, customer, vehicle, and status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting | Created but not yet scheduled |
| Scheduled | Upcoming, not started |
| In Progress | Actively being worked |
| Pending Review | Finished, awaiting review |
| Completed | Done |
| Cancelled | Not happening |
| Rescheduled | Moved to a new date |

No assignments today? The card says so.

<Tip>
  Assignments come from your shop on the web dashboard. Missing a job you expected? Ask your manager.
</Tip>

## Time tracking

The Time card shows two numbers:

| Row | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Hours and minutes clocked so far today |
| **This week** | Total since the start of the week |

A progress bar fills as you approach a standard 40-hour week.

## Messages preview

The messages card on Home previews recent conversations: sender, snippet, and how long ago. An unread count appears when something is waiting.

## Inbox

The full Inbox tab works today. Search and filter conversations, open a thread, start a conversation, and reply where the channel allows it.

## Create and Schedule

Create (new job, estimate, invoice, customer, vehicle) and Schedule are coming soon. Check **My jobs today** on Home for assignments, or use the [web dashboard](https://app.shopwrk.com) for the full calendar and for creating records.

## ShopWrker

The ShopWrker tab opens the assistant for questions about your jobs, customers, or operations. From inside, open **Artifacts** for saved summaries and reports.

<Tip>
  "What are my jobs today?" or "What's the status of the Johnson job?" gets better answers than vague questions.
</Tip>

## Notifications

Push notifications can reach you while the app is closed. Tapping an inbox or assistant notification opens the related screen when ShopWrk can.

Manage delivery in iPhone **Settings → Notifications → ShopWrk**.
