ShopWrk applies your brand everywhere a customer looks: documents, emails, the booking page, and the portal. You manage it all from **Settings → Branding**, across five tabs: **Brand**, **Documents**, **Email**, **Booking Page**, and **Portal**.

<Note>
  The Brand tab is the source of truth. Set your logo, brand color, and email tagline there once and every surface picks them up. The Documents tab holds the numbering, footers, terms, and estimate tier presets shared by invoices and estimates.
</Note>

## Document branding

Open the **Documents** tab to set what invoices and estimates share:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Number prefix">
    One short alphanumeric prefix (up to 10 characters) for both document types. For example, `INV-` produces invoice numbers like `INV-0001`, with the same prefix style applied to estimates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Footer message">
    Up to 500 characters printed at the bottom of every document. Bank details, warranty one-liners, or thank-you notes live here.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Default terms">
    Terms and conditions (up to 2,000 characters) pre-filled on new documents, editable per document when a job needs different language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Estimate tier presets">
    The names and descriptions behind Good / Better / Best quotes. Defaults are Good, Better, Best; rename them, describe each, or add up to five presets for your sales style.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Email branding

Transactional emails (estimate deliveries, invoice notifications, booking confirmations) carry your brand from two places:

- **Brand tab:** the shared logo, brand color, and tagline that form the header.
- **Email tab:** the footer message plus Instagram, Facebook, and website links shown as icons at the bottom of every email.

## Booking page branding

Your public booking page takes its own hero treatment on the **Booking Page** tab. The URL sits at the top of the tab; copy it or click **Preview** to see changes live.

Set a **hero title** and **hero subtitle**, the first words customers read:

- Hero title example: _Premium Auto Styling_
- Hero subtitle example: _Window Tint, PPF, Ceramic Coating & More_

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Accent color | Buttons and highlights on the booking page |
| Logo | Pulled from your Brand tab |
| Background image URL | Optional hero image behind the title |

<Tip>
  Use a high-contrast background photo with a dark overlay so the hero title stays readable. A wide shot of finished work under good light works well.
</Tip>

## Portal branding and access

The **Portal** tab controls what customers see in their [customer portal](/workflows/customer-portal): portal color (falls back to your brand color if empty), logo, welcome message, and how long sessions last.

The same tab carries the switches that matter:

- **Enable Customer Portal.** Off means no customer can sign in.
- **Section visibility.** Choose whether customers see messages, vehicles, warranties, photos, request-quote, and reviews.
- **Session duration.** How long a customer stays signed in after using their magic link.

Save the Portal tab after changing any of these.

## Saving your changes

Each tab has its own **Save Settings** button; nothing applies until you save. Navigate away with unsaved changes and ShopWrk asks before discarding them.
