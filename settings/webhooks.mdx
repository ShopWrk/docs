ShopWrk listens for billing and payment events from Stripe and Finix around the clock: subscription changes, renewal charges, customer payments, payouts, and disputes. You never configure anything here, and there is nothing you need to run. This page explains how updates flow and where to look when something seems stuck.

## How updates reach your dashboard

When something happens, the provider tells ShopWrk immediately:

- **Subscription events** (Stripe) update your [plan status](/settings/billing), renewals, and past-due state.
- **Customer payment events** (ShopWrk Pay) mark invoices paid, record deposits, start payouts, and open disputes. See [ShopWrk Pay](/integrations/shopwrk-pay).

If a delivery hiccups, providers retry automatically, so most transient failures heal themselves within minutes.

## When an update looks stuck

Symptoms: a customer says they paid but the invoice still shows unpaid, a payout has not landed, or your plan status did not change after a renewal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give it fifteen minutes">
    Provider retries usually resolve short outages on their own. Refresh the invoice or billing page before doing anything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the source">
    Look for the matching record in your ShopWrk Pay statements or the Stripe receipt. If the money moved on the provider side, ShopWrk will reconcile it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Still stuck? Contact ShopWrk support with the invoice or payout details and the approximate time it happened. Support can trace the event and replay it safely on our side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not retry a customer's payment yourself because an invoice looks unpaid. Duplicate charges are much harder to unwind than a delayed webhook. Check with support first.
</Warning>
