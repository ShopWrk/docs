Your customer should see your shop name in their inbox. Until you verify a domain, ShopWrk still sends the mail, from a shared ShopWrk address. After you verify, the From line is your shop and your domain, like **Riverside Wrap <hello@riversidewrap.com>**.

This is on Autopilot. Open **Settings**. Under **Communications**, click **Email Domain**. Essential and Pro shops get an upgrade screen. The headline is **Send customer email from your own domain.** Change plan in **Settings → Billing**.

## Add your domain

The page title is **Email Domain**. The card is **Custom sending domain**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a domain you own">
    **Domain** is required. Use your shop site, like `riversidewrap.com`, or a mail host like `mail.riversidewrap.com`. The placeholder is `mail.yourshop.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the From address if you want">
    **From address** is the part before the @. It defaults to `hello`. Leave it unless you already answer mail as `jobs` or `info`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. ShopWrk adds the domain and tells you to add DNS records next. It will not send from that domain yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add the DNS records

After you save, you get a **Sending as** preview, a records table, and **Verification status**.

The table columns are **Type**, **Name / Host**, and **Value**. Copy each row into the DNS settings at the company that hosts your domain. Then click **Check status**.

| Badge | Meaning |
| ----- | ------- |
| **Awaiting DNS** | Records are not confirmed yet. Add them, wait a few minutes, check again. |
| **Verifying…** | ShopWrk is checking right now. |
| **Verified** | Customer email now sends from the preview address. |
| **Failed** | The check did not pass. Fix the records and click **Check status** again. |

When it works, a **Domain verified** box repeats the From line.

ShopWrk never sends from a domain that is still awaiting DNS or failed. That mail keeps using the shared ShopWrk address until the badge says **Verified**.

<Tip>
  DNS can take a little while to catch up after you save records. If **Check status** still says **Awaiting DNS**, wait and try again. Do not delete the domain and start over unless you typed the wrong name.
</Tip>

## What sends from your domain

Once **Verified**, all of this uses that From line:

- Conversation email
- Invoices and estimates
- Booking mail
- Campaigns

Replies still land back in ShopWrk. You do not have to change anything in Conversations.

If you click **Remove domain**, a confirm dialog asks **Remove custom sending domain?** Confirming sends customer email from the shared ShopWrk address again. You can add a domain later.

## Payment and estimate emails customers get

ShopWrk sends these when money or an estimate moves. Each one uses your shop name, logo, and colors from [Branding](/settings/branding). After the domain is verified, they also use your From address.

The message has a button. That button opens the same ShopWrk Pay page, receipt, or estimate ShopWrk already uses for that work.

| When this happens | What they get | Button |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ------ |
| You request a deposit | Deposit request | **Pay deposit** |
| You request a payment | Payment request | **Pay now** |
| They paid and it is still clearing | Payment is processing. Bank transfers can take a few business days. | **View payment** |
| The payment did not go through | Payment unsuccessful | **Try payment again** |
| Payment landed | Receipt | **View receipt** |
| You start a refund | Refund started | **View receipt** |
| The refund is sent | Refund sent | **View receipt** |
| The refund could not finish | Refund issue, asks them to contact you | **Contact shop** |
| An estimate is approved | Estimate approved. If a deposit is due, that amount is in the message. | **Pay deposit** or **View estimate** |
| An estimate is declined | Estimate declined | **Contact shop** |
| You send the final invoice | Invoice is ready | **Pay invoice** |
| They paid part of the balance | Partial payment, remaining amount if ShopWrk has it | **Pay remaining balance** |
| The balance is paid in full | Balance paid | **View invoice** |

ShopWrk does not email them about your own payouts, bank changes, or merchant setup. That stays inside your shop.

<Note>
  A deposit email talks about a deposit. It will not call that amount an invoice.
</Note>

## If mail is not coming from you

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Email Domain page is an upgrade screen">
    You are not on Autopilot. Go to **Settings → Billing** and change plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The badge is still Awaiting DNS or Failed">
    The records in the table have to match what you entered at your DNS provider. Click **Check status** after you save them. Until **Verified**, customer email keeps using the shared ShopWrk address on purpose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Campaigns still look like ShopWrk">
    Campaigns use the same verified domain as invoices and conversations. If the domain is not **Verified**, campaigns stay on the shared address too.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
