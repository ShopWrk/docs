ShopWrker is the assistant built into ShopWrk. You type to it in the same composer on **AI**, the dashboard, onboarding, automations, **Conversations**, and Lead Closer test. When it needs an answer from you, that composer becomes the question. You do not get a second box.

The facts it should keep live in **Settings → ShopWrker**.

## Open ShopWrker settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose ShopWrker">
    Click **ShopWrker**. You get three tabs: **General**, **Memory**, and **MCP**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you still have an old Memory page under **AI**, ShopWrk sends you here now. Bookmark this settings screen instead.

## General

**General** keeps ShopWrker's existing autonomy settings. Those controls were already here. They did not move.

## Memory

**Memory** is the tab you will actually use.

Shop facts are the lasting rules of the shop. Strategy, constraints, preferences, how you operate. They sit in a table. There is no Status column.

Personal notes are yours. They do not become shop-wide rules. Short notes show truncated. Click one to open the editor.

ShopWrker will ask before it changes a shop fact. A customer text does not silently rewrite this list.

Knowledge is still the document library. It is not nested under Memory. Keep price sheets, PDFs, and longer files in Knowledge. Keep the short facts ShopWrker should recite on this tab.

## MCP

**MCP** is coming soon. The tab used to live under Integrations. It is a placeholder for connecting ShopWrker to other tools. Nothing on it is live yet.

## Dashboards

ShopWrker cannot create or edit the live dashboards you use in ShopWrk. **Dashboard → Overview** and **Customize** still work, including dashboards you already saved.

Ask it to make a dashboard and you may get a downloadable page or a PDF. That file is not a dashboard you can pin.
