Your service catalog feeds every estimate and invoice. Build it under **Services** in the main navigation, and configure shared settings in **Settings → Services**.

## How the catalog is organized

Services sit in categories (for example Window Tint, PPF, Ceramic Coating). Inside a category, each service can have options such as Good, Better, and Best. In ShopWrk those options are called **variants**.

Each variant has its own job length and its own prices by **vehicle group**. Vehicle groups are how you price different kinds of cars under that option. Put the dollar amount for each group on the variant. Catalog pricing uses those amounts, not a size multiplier.

You can also create:

- **Packages:** several services sold together at one package price.
- **Combo deals:** mixes across categories (for example tint plus PPF) sold at one bundled price.
- **Add-ons (modifiers):** optional extras on a service, priced as a flat fee, a percent of the service, or a per-panel rate.

## Manage service categories

Categories live under **Settings → Services**.

From that page you can:

- Create a new category
- Rename a category
- Activate or deactivate a category
- Reorder categories so the catalog shows in the order your shop prefers

Deactivated categories stay out of the way when you build new services, without deleting the services already in them.

## Creating a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Services">
    Open **Services** from the sidebar, then click **New Service**. That opens the create wizard at `/services/new`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Pick the category. Categories organize the catalog and control which add-ons show for each service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe the service">
    Enter the name and an optional description. You can use the description helper to draft customer-facing copy from the service name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set technicians and bays">
    On the first step of the wizard, find the **Resources** card (it sits above Deposit and Tax). Pick which technicians and which bays can take this service. Both fields are multiselects. There is no Select all. For a bookable service, choose at least one technician and at least one bay. See [Technicians and bays](#technicians-and-bays) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add variants">
    Each variant is an option the customer can pick, such as Good, Better, or Best. Give each one a clear name, a job duration, and prices for your vehicle groups. Add as many variants as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set vehicle-group prices">
    Inside each variant, enter prices for the vehicle groups your shop uses. Incomplete pricing shows what still needs a number before the quote is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up panels">
    Open the **Panels** tab. If the service has no panels yet, choose **Choose from template** or **Start from scratch**. Use **Manage templates** when you want to edit the shared library first. See [Panel templates](#panel-templates) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure add-ons">
    Open the **Add-ons** tab. Assign modifiers from **Settings → Services → Modifiers** (filtered by category). Mark an add-on as **Suggested** to highlight it while quoting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the service">
    On early wizard steps, click **Continue**. On the last step, click **Save Service** to publish it to your catalog. You can also open an existing service and edit it anytime. The editor stays open after a save so you can keep working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Technicians and bays

Use the **Resources** card when you want to say which people and which bays can take a service. That is eligibility: who and where this work is allowed, not an automatic schedule.

### Where to set it

- **New service:** create wizard step 1 (`/services/new`). Resources sits above Deposit and Tax.
- **Existing service:** open the service, stay on the **Service** tab. Resources sits above Deposit and Tax there too.

### How to choose

Both technicians and bays use an explicit multiselect.

- Pick each technician who should be allowed to take this service.
- Pick each bay where this service can run.
- There is no Select all. You add people and bays one by one.

For a bookable service, select at least one technician and at least one bay.

<Note>
  You can save a service before Resources is complete. An incomplete service is not the same as "anyone" or "any bay." Until you pick people and bays, that service is not set up as bookable eligibility.
</Note>

### New people and bays stay off until you add them

When you hire a technician or add a bay in shop settings, they do **not** automatically join every service. New technicians and new bays stay off a service until someone opens that service (or the create wizard) and adds them on the Resources card.

That keeps specialty work (for example ceramic only, or one bay for paint protection) from silently opening to everyone.

### How this connects to booking

Later, when someone books the service by hand, they choose within the technicians and bays you configured on that service. Resources is the allow-list for that service. It does not invent a schedule or force auto-assignment beyond that eligibility.

### Walkthrough: limit ceramic coating to two techs and one bay

1. Open **Services** and click the ceramic coating service (or **New Service** if you are building it).
2. On the Service tab (or create wizard step 1), scroll to **Resources** above Deposit and Tax.
3. In technicians, select the two people who actually run ceramic jobs.
4. In bays, select the one bay with the right lighting and space.
5. Leave other technicians and bays unselected.
6. Click **Continue** through the remaining create steps, or **Save** / **Save Service** if you are editing.
7. Hire a new tech later: they will not appear as eligible for this ceramic service until you come back and add them on Resources.

## Variants and vehicle-group prices

ShopWrk quotes with package options such as Good / Better / Best. Each option is a **variant** on a service.

Under each variant, **vehicle groups** are the prices for different vehicle types. Put the dollar amounts you actually charge for that group on that variant. ShopWrk uses those amounts on estimates and jobs.

Keep one pricing story for the shop: variants for the package level, vehicle groups for the car.

## Panel templates

Panels are the parts of the vehicle a service covers (for example windshield, driver door, or a ceramic panel). Templates let you reuse a panel layout across services.

### Manage the template library

Open the panel template library from **Settings**, or click **Manage templates** from a service **Panels** tab. Create, edit, or delete templates there. The first time you open it, ShopWrk adds starter templates you can edit like any other row.

When you create a template, you can say whether panels ask for a VLT shade. Inside the template editor, turn shade on or off per panel so mixed tint and non-tint templates work.

### Apply templates on a service

In the service editor **Panels** tab:

- An empty panel list shows **Choose from template** and **Start from scratch**, plus **Manage templates**.
- After panels exist, manage them as usual. Use **Apply template…** when you want to replace the current list (ShopWrk asks you to confirm).
- On a variant, you can copy panels from another variant, add panels from the pool or a template, or reset the variant panels back to the chooser (with confirm).

VLT shade controls show only on panels that ask for a shade. Tint and ceramic panels can live on the same service.

## Service packages

A package groups several services into one line item on an estimate or invoice. The package price can differ from the sum of the parts.

Create one under **Services → Packages → New Package**, pick the services, and set the package price.

## Combo deals

Combos combine services from different categories, for example window tint plus PPF, at a bundled price. You can set combo prices by variant and by vehicle group.

Create one under **Services → Combos → New Combo**, choose the services, and set pricing for each option and vehicle group.

## Add-ons and modifiers

Modifiers are optional extras staff or customers can attach to a service on an estimate. Manage them in **Settings → Services → Modifiers**. Each modifier has:

- A name and optional description
- A pricing type: flat fee, percentage of the service price, or per-panel rate
- An optional category filter. Leave blank to offer it on all services.

After a modifier exists, assign it to services from the **Add-ons** tab in the service editor.

## Tax configuration

Go to **Settings → Services** to set tax mode:

- **Tax added to price (exclusive):** listed prices get tax added at checkout.
- **Tax included in price (inclusive):** listed prices already include tax; ShopWrk back-calculates the net amount.

Mark individual services as taxable or not inside the service editor. On the Service tab and create wizard step 1, Deposit and Tax sit below the Resources card.

<Note>
  Changing tax mode affects new estimates and invoices only. Existing documents stay as they were.
</Note>

## Deposits

You can require a deposit per service. In the service editor (or create wizard step 1), turn on **Deposit required** and choose a flat dollar amount or a percentage of the service price. That setting flows through to estimates and invoices for that service. The Deposit controls sit below Resources on the Service tab.
