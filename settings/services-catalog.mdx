Your service catalog feeds every estimate and invoice. Build it under **Services** in the main navigation, and configure shared settings in **Settings → Services**.

## How the catalog is organized

Services sit in categories (for example Window Tint, PPF, Ceramic Coating). Inside a category, each service can have tiers such as Good, Better, and Best. In ShopWrk those tiers are called **variants**. Each tier has its own price and job length, and can break out by vehicle size.

You can also create:

- **Packages:** several services sold together at one package price.
- **Combo deals:** mixes across categories (for example tint plus PPF) sold at one bundled price.
- **Add-ons (modifiers):** optional extras on a service, priced as a flat fee, a percent of the service, or a per-panel rate.

## Creating a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Services">
    Open **Services** from the sidebar, then click **New Service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Pick the category. Categories organize the catalog and control which add-ons show for each service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe the service">
    Enter the name and an optional description. You can use the description helper to draft customer-facing copy from the service name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a pricing mode">
    Pick how this service prices different vehicle sizes:

    - **Flat vehicle prices:** enter exact prices for Sedan/Coupe, SUV/Crossover, and Large SUV/Truck on each variant.
    - **Multiplier pricing:** set a base price on each variant, then multiply by vehicle size (for example 1.0× sedan, 1.2× SUV, 1.4× large).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add variants (tiers)">
    Each variant is a tier of the service, such as Good, Better, or Best. For each one set a name, duration, and price (flat by vehicle size, or a base price used with multipliers). Add as many variants as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure add-ons">
    After saving, open the **Add-ons** tab. Assign modifiers from **Settings → Services → Modifiers** (filtered by category). Mark an add-on as **Suggested** to highlight it while quoting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the service">
    Click **Save** to publish it to your catalog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier-based pricing by vehicle size

ShopWrk quotes with Good / Better / Best packages. Each package level is a variant on a service. Each variant can have its own price for each vehicle size class.

**Flat vehicle pricing** gives the most control. You enter exact dollar amounts for sedan, SUV, and large on every variant. Use this when prices do not follow a clean pattern across sizes.

**Multiplier pricing** is faster to maintain. You set one base price per variant and ShopWrk multiplies it by a vehicle-size factor. Default multipliers live in **Settings → Services** and apply shop-wide unless you override them on a service or variant.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Default vehicle multipliers">
    Shop-wide defaults live in Settings → Services. Factory defaults are 1.0× sedan, 1.2× SUV, and 1.4× large. Change them to match how your shop prices larger vehicles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Service-level multiplier overrides">
    Turn on **Override** in a service's pricing settings to set different multipliers for that service only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Duration multipliers">
    You can also multiply job duration by vehicle size so larger vehicles block the right amount of calendar time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Service packages

A package groups several services into one line item on an estimate or invoice. The package price can differ from the sum of the parts.

Create one under **Services → Packages → New Package**, pick the services, and set the package price.

## Combo deals

Combos combine services from different categories, for example window tint plus PPF, at a bundled price. You can set combo prices by tier (Good, Better, Best) and by vehicle size.

Create one under **Services → Combos → New Combo**, choose the services, and set pricing for each tier and vehicle size.

## Add-ons and modifiers

Modifiers are optional extras staff or customers can attach to a service on an estimate. Manage them in **Settings → Services → Modifiers**. Each modifier has:

- A name and optional description
- A pricing type: flat fee, percentage of the service price, or per-panel rate
- An optional category filter. Leave blank to offer it on all services.

After a modifier exists, assign it to services from the **Add-ons** tab in the service editor.

## Tax configuration

Go to **Settings → Services** to set tax mode:

- **Tax added to price (exclusive):** listed prices get tax added at checkout.
- **Tax included in price (inclusive):** listed prices already include tax; ShopWrk back-calculates the net amount.

Mark individual services as taxable or not inside the service editor.

<Note>
  Changing tax mode affects new estimates and invoices only. Existing documents stay as they were.
</Note>

## Deposits

You can require a deposit per service. In the service editor, turn on **Deposit required** and choose a flat dollar amount or a percentage of the service price. That setting flows through to estimates and invoices for that service.
