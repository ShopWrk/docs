If your shop texts customers in the United States, carriers require you to register as a legitimate business sender under a system called A2P 10DLC. Unregistered numbers get their messages filtered or blocked, usually without any error on your end. The customer just never receives the text.

Registration is a one-time setup inside ShopWrk. It takes about ten minutes; carrier approval takes a few business days.

## What registration covers

There are four stages:

1. **Brand registration** tells carriers who your business is: legal name, address, and tax ID (EIN).
2. **Vetting** checks the submitted business information against carrier records.
3. **Campaign registration** describes how you text: appointment reminders, estimate follow-ups, promotions, and similar customer messaging.
4. **Number assignment** attaches your approved campaign to your ShopWrk phone number.

You do all of this from **Settings → Messaging**, starting with **Start A2P registration**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open A2P Registration">
    Go to **Settings → Messaging** and click **Start A2P registration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your business details">
    Use the exact legal name and EIN from your tax documents. A mismatch between your business name and EIN is the most common reason brands get rejected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your messaging">
    Pick the use cases that match what your shop sends, like appointments and estimates. Sample messages are pre-filled based on the templates shops actually use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    ShopWrk files everything with the carriers and tracks status on the same page. Most registrations clear within a few business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can receive inbound texts while registration is pending. Outbound business SMS unlocks once both Brand and Campaign show **Approved**, so start registration early; the rest of your setup can proceed in parallel.
</Info>

## What it costs

Registration fees are billed separately from your subscription and appear on your [billing page](/settings/billing):

| Fee | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Brand registration | \$5.40 one-time |
| Campaign registration | \$18.00 one-time |
| Medium Volume Mixed upgrade | \$12/month, only if you need higher throughput |

Most shops never need the Medium Volume upgrade. It exists for high-volume senders; the default low-volume tier handles typical reminder and follow-up traffic fine.

## If registration gets rejected

The A2P page shows the carrier's rejection reason verbatim. The usual culprits:

- **Brand rejected:** legal name does not match the EIN record. Fix the name exactly as it appears on IRS documents and resubmit.
- **Campaign rejected:** sample messages looked promotional when the campaign was filed as reminders, or vice versa. Adjust the use case or samples and resubmit.

Rejections are common and resubmission is free. Nothing about your phone number changes during the process.

<Note>
  This page covers US A2P 10DLC registration. Texting customers outside the United States through a ShopWrk number requires talking to support first; requirements differ by country.
</Note>
