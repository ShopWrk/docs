---
title: "Create service checklists and complete them on jobs"
description: "Build reusable templates in Settings, attach one published checklist to each service, and check off the copy that lands on each job."
---

A checklist is the step list your techs follow for a service. You keep templates in Settings. You attach the live checklist to the service. Each new job gets its own copy of whatever is published on that service.

## Open your template library

Go to **Settings → Checklist templates**. This page is your shop's template library.

Templates are starting points you copy from. They are not assigned to a service on their own.

On this page you will see usage counts such as **Used on N services**. Click **New template** to open the full-screen editor. Click a template row to edit it. You can duplicate a template. Delete stays disabled while a template is already in use.

<Note>
  ShopWrk no longer keeps a separate shop-wide checklist editor under Settings. Links that used to open one now return you to this library. Build the live checklist on the service.
</Note>

## Attach a checklist to a service

Each saved service owns one checklist. If the service has variants, they share that same checklist. You do not assign a checklist to a job type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the service first">
    Go to **Services** and open the service. If you are creating a new service, click **Save** before you add a checklist. ShopWrk will not attach a checklist to an unsaved service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Checklist card">
    On the service, find the **Checklist** card. It shows whether the checklist is published, still a draft, or not added yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create blank or use a template">
    Click **Create blank** to start empty, or **Use template** to copy a template into a draft on this service. **Edit** opens the full-page checklist editor for that service. **Remove** takes the checklist off the service.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New jobs only copy a published checklist. A draft stays on the service until you publish it. If the service has no published checklist, the job will show **No checklist has been added to this job.**

The old checklist tab on the service is gone. Use the Checklist card.

<Tip>
  Change the service checklist when your process changes. Jobs you already created keep the copy they received.
</Tip>

## What a new job copies

When you click **Convert to Job** on an estimate, or **New Job** on the calendar, ShopWrk copies the published checklist from each service onto that job. That copy does not change if you later edit the service.

## Complete items and view history

Open the job detail page. Complete items there as work gets done. Customers do not see the checklist.

Required photo or video steps must be finished before you can mark the job complete. Optional steps never block completion.

If a step is later removed, it still appears under **Removed steps**. History stays on the job. Removed steps do not count toward progress.
