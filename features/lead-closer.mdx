Lead Closer texts your customers about wrap, tint, and PPF work. It answers from your catalog, can take a ShopWrk Pay deposit, and books a bay after they pay. It is not the chat in the sidebar. That chat helps you run the shop. Lead Closer talks to the customer.

Open **Agents**, then **Lead Closer**.

**Overview** is the setup chat. **Configure** is where you set Response Mode, turn services on, and edit Playbooks. **Knowledge** and **Learnings** sit next to each other. **Analytics** is its own tab.

<Info>
  Live Lead Closer needs ShopWrk Pay on, a shop phone number you own, and approved business texting. Lead Closer is on the [Pro plan](/settings/billing). See Billing if texting registration is still open.
</Info>

## Test vs live

Test uses your unpublished settings. It does not text a real customer. It does not take a deposit, book a bay, or write live deals. A deal is the job you are trying to book with that customer. Test data is kept for 30 days, then dropped.

Use Test like you would ride along with a new closer. Mark a reply **Helpful** or **Not helpful**, or suggest a better reply. That files work for review. It does not change what live customers see.

**Restart** starts the Test customer over.

## Publish

When the draft is right, **Publish**. Publish shows what changed, what is blocked, and what is true in the shop right now. Then it turns the whole draft on at once.

Rollback publishes an older version again. It does not unsend texts, undo payments, or pull jobs off the [calendar](/features/calendar-booking).

If you have not run Test, leave Auto off.

## Playbooks, Knowledge, and Learnings

A **Playbook** is how you want that service sold. Goals, common questions, objections, proof, and follow-up notes live here. Start from a blank page, a ShopWrk starter, or a shop template. Editing a template does not rewrite Playbooks you already attached to a service.

Shop prices, coverage, deposits, and schedule always beat Playbook text. If you teach an answer that fights the Playbook, that answer can help this customer now. The Playbook change waits. You will see **Current** versus **Proposed**. **Publish proposal** is separate from **Publish Playbook**.

**Knowledge** is shop-wide. Warranties, house rules, and answers that are not tied to one service. Click **Add**. Knowledge is a fallback when the Playbook does not cover the question. It cannot set a price or authorize a booking.

**Learnings** is the review list beside Knowledge. Proposed reply changes show up here with their source and which replies they would affect. **Approve** activates a change. **Reject** throws it out. **Revert** turns off guidance that is already live. Teaching a better answer in a conversation or in Test files a candidate. Live texts do not change until you review it.

## Auto, Draft, and Off

**Response Mode** is how much Lead Closer is allowed to do on live texts.

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Off | No reply and no draft. |
| Draft | Writes a reply in the composer. Does not send. Does not create contacts, deals, payment requests, or bookings. |
| Auto | Can send and close, only inside the settings you have Published, and only when the shop is ready for that work. |

The first time you Publish, you pick Off, Draft, or Auto. Later Publishes keep the mode you already have unless you change it yourself.

A payment that already came in still processes if Response Mode is Off. What the customer is told follows the mode. Off sends nothing. Draft writes composer text. Auto can send the confirmation.

## Services: Triage or Close

On Configure, use **Add service**, then open the service. Pick **Triage** or **Close**. You can also choose **Do not offer**.

**Triage** means Lead Closer can talk about the service and collect what you need. It cannot quote a price, send a payment link, or book.

**Close** means it can take the customer through agreement, a ShopWrk Pay deposit, and booking.

Variants are the size or option add-ons on that service. Include or exclude them. You do not set Triage or Close on a variant by itself.

If a service is missing price, deposit rules, a Playbook, or a free bay and installer, Lead Closer will not pretend it is ready. Close does not fall back to Triage.

## Handoff in Conversations

Live texts live in [Conversations](/features/communications). If the customer needs a shop answer Lead Closer does not have, Conversations pauses Lead Closer on that thread.

Click **Add the answer** to teach the missing answer, then **Continue**, then choose who sends the text. Click **I'll reply myself** if you want to write it. The thread switches to **You're replying**. **Let Lead Closer reply again** puts it back on pause until you finish teaching or send.

If you send while Lead Closer would still reply, you get **Send and take over** as the default, **Send and keep AI active**, or **Cancel**. Keep AI active leaves your message in the thread. Lead Closer waits for the next customer text.

If the work is outside your services, mixed with work you do not offer, or something Lead Closer must not handle, it stops for that whole contact. It does not tell the customer that a person is taking over.

Resume is something you do on purpose. There is no timer that hands the thread back.

## Follow-ups

Follow-up count and the no-response lost stage live under Configure, **Advanced**, then **Shop policy**. You can set zero to six follow-up tries. Zero turns follow-ups off. Follow-ups above zero need a pipeline and a lost stage before Publish will go through. The pipeline is the stages a deal moves through. The lost stage is the one you use when a lead goes quiet.

Auto sends follow-ups inside your outbound window. Draft writes a follow-up in the composer and will not overwrite text you already typed.

After the last try, Auto marks that deal lost for no response. Draft only recommends. If the customer texts back later, the deal can come back. A new inbound cancels a live follow-up.

## ShopWrk Pay deposit, then schedule

Close does not drop an invoice link in the thread.

After the customer agrees, ShopWrk saves the agreement and the time they asked for. The product calls that a **Booking Intent**. It does not hold the bay yet.

Then ShopWrk Pay issues one deposit link that lasts 30 days. The amount is locked at agreement from your catalog. If the job changes, the old link is revoked and a new one is issued.

When the deposit is paid, ShopWrk checks that a bay and a qualified installer are still free, then schedules the [job](/workflows/jobs) on the [calendar](/features/calendar-booking). If you Close with no deposit, the same scheduling step runs right after agreement.

Payment can land after someone else took the slot. The money is still captured. The job is not booked. A person on your shop has to pick it up. Lead Closer will not tell the customer they are booked.

Google Calendar sync is extra. If Google fails, the ShopWrk booking still stands.

<Warning>
  A 30-day deposit link does not hold the bay. Customers pay when they are ready. The schedule is checked at payment, not at the moment you sent the link.
</Warning>

## Analytics

Open the **Analytics** tab on Lead Closer. Numbers come from real paid deposits and collected revenue on booked jobs, not from sample data. No-deposit bookings are not counted as primary revenue.

You will see revenue, which services produced it, where leads stop, what needs attention, and results by day.
