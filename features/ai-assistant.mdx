ShopWrk includes an AI assistant built specifically for auto styling shops. It knows your customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, and inventory — so instead of asking generic questions, you can ask things like "What did I tint for this customer last year?" or "Draft a follow-up SMS for my open estimates this week." Open **AI** from the sidebar to start a conversation.

## What the AI assistant can do

The assistant has access to tools that let it read and act on your shop's data. You can ask it to:

- Look up a customer's vehicle history or lifetime spend
- Summarize open estimates or unpaid invoices
- Draft SMS messages, emails, or social media posts
- Help you build promotional offers or packages
- Walk you through how to use a ShopWrk feature
- Analyze your job volume, revenue trends, or popular services
- Coach you on sales conversations and objection handling

<Info>
  The assistant can read your shop's data to give you accurate, specific answers. It cannot make changes to your data unless you explicitly ask it to perform an action and confirm.
</Info>

## Accessing the assistant

Click **AI** in the left sidebar to open the chat interface. Each conversation is saved so you can pick up where you left off. Click the pencil icon or press **Cmd+K** (Mac) / **Ctrl+K** (Windows) to start a new conversation.

## Knowledge base

The knowledge base is a library of documents the AI can reference when answering your questions. Use it to store information specific to your shop:

- Pricing guides and package details
- Manufacturer warranty terms
- Film spec sheets and install notes
- Staff training materials
- Frequently asked questions from customers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the knowledge base">
    Go to **AI → Knowledge Base** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a document">
    Click **Add Document** and upload a file or paste text content. Give it a descriptive title so the assistant can find it when relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions that reference it">
    Once a document is in the knowledge base, the assistant will automatically include it in its context when you ask related questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you first set up the AI assistant, ShopWrk will prompt you to go through a knowledge base onboarding step. This helps seed the assistant with the most useful shop-specific information right away.
</Note>

## Tips for better results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be specific about what you want">
    Instead of "help me with my customers," try "show me the 5 customers who spent the most in the last 90 days." The more specific your question, the more useful the answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask follow-up questions">
    The assistant remembers the conversation context. If you ask for a list and want more detail on one item, just ask — you don't need to repeat the full context.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use it for drafts, not final copy">
    The AI is excellent at generating first drafts of SMS messages, emails, and social posts. Always review and personalize the output before sending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check generated numbers">
    When the assistant pulls data from your shop (revenue totals, job counts, etc.), treat the numbers as a starting point and verify anything important in the corresponding report.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
