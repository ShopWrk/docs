ShopWrk includes an AI assistant built for wrap, tint, and PPF shops. It knows your customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, and inventory. You can ask "What did I tint for this customer last year?" or "Draft a follow-up SMS for my open estimates this week." Open **AI** from the sidebar to start a conversation.

## What the AI assistant can do

The assistant can look up your shop's data and take actions you confirm. You can ask it to:

- Look up a customer's vehicle history or lifetime spend
- Summarize open estimates or unpaid invoices
- Draft SMS messages, emails, or social posts
- Help you build promotional offers or packages
- Walk you through how to use a ShopWrk feature
- Analyze your job volume, revenue trends, or popular services
- Coach you on sales conversations and objection handling

<Info>
  The assistant can read your shop's data to give you specific answers. It will not change customers, jobs, or invoices unless you ask it to and confirm.
</Info>

## Open the assistant

Click **AI** in the left sidebar. The chat opens. Each conversation is saved, so you can pick up where you left off. Click the pencil icon to start a new conversation.

**Cmd\+K** on a Mac, or **Ctrl\+K** on Windows, opens shop search. It does not start a new chat. See [shop search](/features/command-palette). From search, **Quick AI** can hand the same thread into this full chat with **Open in full chat**.

## Knowledge base

The knowledge base is a library of documents the AI can use when answering. Store shop-specific information here:

- Pricing guides and package details
- Manufacturer warranty terms
- Film spec sheets and install notes
- Staff training materials
- Frequently asked questions from customers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the knowledge base">
    Go to **AI → Knowledge Base** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a document">
    Click **Add Document** and upload a file or paste text. Give it a descriptive title so the assistant can find it when it matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions that use it">
    Once a document is in the knowledge base, the assistant includes it when you ask related questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you first set up the AI assistant, ShopWrk will prompt you to add knowledge base documents. Start with pricing, film notes, and the shop details the assistant should know.
</Note>

## Tips for better results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be specific about what you want">
    Instead of "help me with my customers," try "show me the 5 customers who spent the most in the last 90 days." The more specific your question, the more useful the answer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ask follow-up questions">
    The assistant remembers the conversation. If you ask for a list and want more detail on one item, just ask. You do not need to repeat the full context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use it for drafts, not final copy">
    The assistant is good at first drafts of SMS messages, emails, and social posts. Always review and personalize the output before sending.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Check generated numbers">
    When the assistant pulls data from your shop, treat the numbers as a starting point. Verify anything important in the matching report.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
