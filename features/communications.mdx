Every conversation your shop has with a customer lands in one place: their texts, Instagram and Facebook DMs, and call activity, all threaded on the customer's profile. Anyone on your team can pick up any thread with full context. Nobody has to ask whose phone the conversation is on.

Open **Conversations** from the sidebar.

<Note>
  Connect at least one messaging channel to start new conversations. For SMS, that means claiming a number and completing A2P registration. Instagram and Facebook messaging uses a Meta connection instead.
</Note>

## The unified inbox

The thread list shows every customer conversation across channels, with filters for All, SMS, Calls, and Unread. Each thread groups everything with that contact: SMS, social messages, call recordings with transcripts, and internal notes. Reply from wherever you are in the thread and it goes out over the channel the customer used.

<Info>
  Instagram and Facebook messages appear here after you connect your Meta accounts. See [Connect Facebook and Instagram](/integrations/meta). Until then, manage those conversations in the Meta apps.
</Info>

## Sending messages

Open a thread or start a new one from any contact. Type in the composer and send. Attachments work: photos of film samples, panel shots, before-and-after pictures, voice notes.

Templates cover the messages you type twice a day: appointment reminders, estimate notifications, payment links, aftercare instructions. Insert one from the composer, or manage the library under **Settings → Messaging → Templates**.

### Drafting with AI

When the lead closer is enabled, the composer can draft replies from the conversation context: what the customer asked, what they drive, what you quoted. You review, edit, and send, or turn on autopilot per lead source and let it reply within the rules you set. See [AI agents](/features/ai-agents).

## Voice calls

Calling runs through the dashboard, no desk phone needed:

- **Outbound.** Click the phone icon on any contact or thread. The call connects through your browser and logs to the customer's history when you hang up.
- **Incoming.** Calls to your shop's number ring in the dashboard with caller ID and, if enabled, a screen-pop of who is calling and their job history. Miss them and voicemail catches it.
- **Recording and transcripts.** When recording is on, calls are recorded and transcribed with an AI summary: key topics, action items, next steps. Review twenty calls in the time it takes to listen to one.

<Warning>
  Call recording consent laws differ by state. Know yours before turning recording on.
</Warning>

Routing options live under **Settings → Calling**: business hours, forwarding rules, per-lead handling (autopilot, answer-only, or route to a specific person), and voicemail greetings.

## Email

One-off emails send from the same thread as everything else. For reaching many customers at once, that is a [campaign](/features/campaigns), not a conversation.

## Opt-outs and quiet hours

Customers who reply STOP are opted out of SMS, including later manual texts sent from ShopWrk. Other channels follow their own consent and delivery rules. Automated sends also respect quiet hours: scheduled messages hold until morning in the customer's timezone rather than buzzing anyone at midnight.
