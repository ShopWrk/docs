ShopWrk puts customer texts, calls, and email in one place. From **Conversations** you can text a customer, place a call, and keep the history on their profile.

<Note>
  Conversations needs messaging set up for your shop. If you see a "Set Up Messaging" prompt, go to **Settings → Messaging** to connect your phone number and configure SMS.
</Note>

## Conversations

**Conversations** lists your customer threads. A thread groups texts, calls, and notes with that person so you have the context before you reply.

Call threads show up here as soon as you start dialing, and inbound calls land here too. You do not need recording turned on for the call to appear.

## SMS messaging

ShopWrk texts come from your shop's registered phone number and arrive in the customer's regular messaging app.

### Sending a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Click a customer's thread in Conversations, or click **\+ New Conversation** to start a thread with a contact who does not have one yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose your message">
    Type your message in the composer at the bottom of the thread. You can type freely or insert a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press Enter or click **Send**. The message appears in the thread and is delivered to the customer's phone number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Message templates

Templates save messages you send often, like appointment reminders, estimate notices, and payment links, so you are not typing them from scratch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open templates">
    In the message composer, click the **Templates** icon, or go to **Settings → Messaging → Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template">
    Select a template from the list. It fills the composer. Edit the text if you need to before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a new template">
    In Settings, click **New Template**, name it, write the body, and save. It is immediately available in the composer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Bulk messaging

Send one SMS to many contacts at once. This is useful for promotions, reminders, or seasonal campaigns.

<Warning>
  Bulk SMS is subject to A2P 10DLC rules. Complete A2P registration before sending bulk messages. See [A2P 10DLC](#a2p-10dlc) below.
</Warning>

### A2P 10DLC

A2P 10DLC registration is required by US carriers for business SMS. Without it, your messages may be filtered or blocked.

ShopWrk guides you through registration from **Settings → Messaging → A2P Registration**. You provide your business name, EIN, and a description of how you use texts. Approval usually takes a few business days.

<Info>
  A2P registration is a one-time setup. Once approved, your shop number is treated as a business sender and deliverability is better.
</Info>

## Voice calls

You can make and receive ShopWrk calls from Chrome. A Work Phone can ring at the same time. Set the route at **Settings → Calling**. See [Call Flow](/integrations/sms-voice#call-flow) for the full setup.

### Making a call

Start a call from a customer thread in Conversations. As soon as dialing begins, ShopWrk creates a call thread for that number. A saved customer is linked. An unknown number still gets a visible call thread.

While the call is live, Conversations shows Calling, Ringing, or On a call. Hangup, no answer, and a completed talk all stay on that thread.

### Receiving calls

Incoming calls to your shop number ring every ready Chrome tab and the Primary Recipient's Work Phone together. The incoming-call screen shows on each ready tab, not only one of them.

Answer in Chrome, or press 1 on the Work Phone when you hear "ShopWork business call. Press 1 to accept." The first accepted destination wins. If nobody answers, the caller reaches ShopWrk voicemail.

On the phone the spoken brand is ShopWork. Written ShopWrk stays ShopWrk.

### Voicemail

Missed calls go to ShopWrk voicemail. The message is stored on the conversation thread so you can play it back from Conversations.

### Call recording

Recording is not automatic for every call. The shop toggle on **Settings → Calling** is the only start policy.

When recording is on, ShopWrk starts after a person answers. You can play the audio back on the call thread in Conversations. ShopWrk then transcribes the call and writes a summary. If those fail, the audio and the call history still stay.

When recording is off, you still get the call history in Conversations. There is no audio and no transcript or summary.

<Warning>
  Recording rules differ by state. ShopWrk plays a disclosure when recording is on. Know the consent rules where you operate.
</Warning>

### Call Flow

Owners and admins set Call Flow at **Settings → Calling**. Choose a Primary Recipient, Work Phone, fallback teammates, and an optional fallback number. Save, then reload. The same route should still be selected.

A missing or inactive Call Flow sends callers to ShopWrk voicemail instead of leaving the call hanging.

## Email

ShopWrk sends transactional emails, such as estimates, invoices, and job confirmations, automatically. You can also send one-off emails to customers and run targeted email campaigns.

### Email campaigns

Use campaigns to send a message to a specific list of contacts. For example, all customers who have not been in for more than 6 months, or everyone tagged "Window Tint".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Marketing">
    Navigate to **Marketing** in the sidebar and go to the **Email** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a campaign">
    Click **New Campaign**, choose a name, and select your audience by filtering contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your email">
    Compose the subject line and body. You can use personalization tokens like the customer's first name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Send immediately or schedule the campaign for a later date and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delivery tracking

After sending a campaign, ShopWrk tracks delivery status for each recipient: delivered, opened, or bounced. View these stats from the campaign detail page.
