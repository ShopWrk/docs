ShopWrk puts customer texts, calls, and email in one place. From **Conversations** you can text a customer, place a call, and keep the history on their profile.

<Note>
  Conversations needs a shop number. If you see **Set Up Messaging**, finish that on **Settings → Messaging**. See [Texts and calling](/integrations/sms-voice).
</Note>

## Conversations

**Conversations** lists your customer threads. A thread groups texts, calls, and notes with that person so you have the context before you reply.

CALL threads show up here as soon as you start dialing. Inbound calls land here too. You do not need recording turned on for the call to appear.

## Send a text

ShopWrk texts come from your shop number and arrive in the customer's regular messaging app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Click a customer's thread in Conversations, or click **+ New Conversation** to start a thread with a contact who does not have one yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose your message">
    Type in the composer at the bottom of the thread. You can type freely or insert a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press Enter or click **Send**. The message appears in the thread and goes to the customer's phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Message templates

Templates save messages you send often, like appointment reminders, estimate notices, and ShopWrk Pay links.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open templates">
    In the message composer, click the **Templates** icon, or go to **Settings → Messaging → Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template">
    Select a template from the list. It fills the composer. Edit it before sending if you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a new template">
    In Settings, click **New Template**, name it, write the body, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To text many customers at once, see [bulk texts](/integrations/sms-voice#bulk-text-campaigns). Email to a list is [Campaigns](/features/campaigns).

## Calls

Make and receive ShopWrk calls from Chrome. A Work Phone can ring at the same time. Set the route at **Settings → Calling**. Full setup is [Call Flow](/integrations/sms-voice#call-flow).

### Make a call

Open a customer thread in Conversations, or open the customer, then click the phone icon.

As soon as dialing begins, ShopWrk creates a CALL thread for that number. A saved customer is linked. An unknown number still gets a visible CALL thread.

While the call is live, Conversations shows Calling, Ringing, or On a call. Hangup, no answer, and a completed talk all stay on that thread.

### Answer a call

Incoming calls to your shop number ring ready Chrome tabs and the Primary Recipient's Work Phone together. Answer in Chrome, or press 1 on the Work Phone. The first accepted destination wins. If nobody answers, the caller reaches ShopWrk voicemail.

That inbound call still gets a CALL thread in Conversations, missed or answered.

Ring rules, fallbacks, and Work Phone live on [Texts and calling](/integrations/sms-voice).

### Voicemail

Missed calls go to ShopWrk voicemail. Play the message back from the conversation thread.

### Call recording

Recording is off until you turn it on at **Settings → Calling**. When it is on, open the CALL thread in Conversations to play the audio. ShopWrk then writes a transcript and a summary on that thread. If those fail, the audio and the call history still stay.

When recording is off, you still get the CALL history. There is no audio, transcript, or summary.

<Warning>
  Recording rules differ by state. ShopWrk plays a disclosure when recording is on. Know the consent rules where you operate.
</Warning>

## Email

ShopWrk sends estimates, invoices, and job confirmations on its own. One-off email lives on the customer's thread. A blast to a list is [Campaigns](/features/campaigns). To send from your shop domain, see [Sending domain](/settings/sending-domain).
