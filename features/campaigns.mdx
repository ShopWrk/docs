New leads cost money. Campaigns make money from the customers already in your CRM: winter ceramic specials to last summer's coating customers, a reminder to everyone whose warranty expires soon, a win-back offer to clients who went quiet. You build the audience from real shop data and send to all of them at once.

Open **Marketing** from the sidebar.

## Building a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click **New Campaign**. Give it a name your team will recognize in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the audience">
    Filter contacts by tag, service history, last visit date, vehicle type, or lead source. For example: customers tagged Ceramic who haven't booked in 6 months.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Compose the email or SMS. Personalization tokens drop in the customer's name, vehicle, or last service automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Send now, or schedule for when your customers actually read messages. Then watch delivered, opened, clicked, and bounced counts roll in per recipient.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Let the assistant draft it. Ask for "a win-back SMS for ceramic customers we haven't seen since spring" in the AI chat, then paste the winner into your campaign. See [AI agents](/features/ai-agents).
</Tip>

## Segments that earn their keep

| Segment | Built from | Typical offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lapsed ceramic | Last visit 6\+ months, coating job history | Annual inspection plus booster top-up |
| Winter prep | Tag or service history | Fall booking discount before the rush |
| Quote ghosts | Estimates sent, never approved | Limited deposit-match nudge |
| One-time tints | Exactly one completed job | Full-front PPF upgrade intro |
| Fleet accounts | Company contacts | Quarterly maintenance reminder |

Save any filter set as a segment and it refreshes as your data changes, so the same campaign can rerun each season without rebuilding the list.

## Compliance basics

- Email campaigns require no special setup. Unsubscribe links are handled for you.
- SMS campaigns go through your registered A2P number. Complete [A2P registration](/settings/a2p-registration) before your first text blast.
- Customers who opted out are excluded automatically, and opt-outs are honored across every channel.
- Quiet hours apply to scheduled sends. ShopWrk holds messages until morning in the customer's timezone rather than texting someone at midnight.

<Note>
  A good rule of thumb: email monthly at most, SMS only when there's a reason. Shops that respect the channel keep their lists responsive; shops that hammer them train customers to ignore everything.
</Note>
