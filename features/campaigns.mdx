New leads cost money. Email campaigns make money from the customers already in your CRM: a winter ceramic special to last summer's coating customers, a heads-up to everyone whose warranty inspection is due, a win-back offer to clients who went quiet. Build the audience from real shop data, write once, send to all of them.

Email campaigns are on the **Autopilot** plan. Open **Marketing** from the sidebar. Essential and Pro shops see an upgrade screen. Change plan in **Settings → Billing**.

For your shop texting number and Call Flow, see [Texts and calling](/integrations/sms-voice). That page is for the shop line, not email blasts.

## Building an email campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click **New Campaign**. Give it a name your team will recognize in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the audience">
    Choose from built-in audience types: all contacts, new in the last 30 days, inactive for 90 or more days, high spenders, by service, by vehicle, by tag, or a custom recipient list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the email">
    Add the subject, preview text, and content. The customer's first name and similar details drop in automatically, and a preview shows the result before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review recipients">
    Check the recipient preview before sending, especially after narrowing the list. Contacts who cannot receive the campaign are excluded automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Send now or schedule for when your customers read email. Delivered, opened, clicked, and bounced counts roll in per recipient afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Let the assistant draft it. Ask for "a win-back email for ceramic customers we haven't seen since spring" in the AI chat, then paste the winner into your campaign. See [AI agents](/features/ai-agents).
</Tip>

## Audiences that earn their keep

| Audience | Built from | Typical offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lapsed ceramic | Inactive 90\+ days plus coating history | Annual inspection with booster top-up |
| New since spring | New in last 30 days, filtered by tag | First-visit PPF intro pricing |
| Quote ghosts | Custom list from estimates sent, never approved | Limited deposit-match nudge |
| High spenders | High spenders preset | Early access to seasonal booking |

Preview the recipient count each time; audiences snapshot at send rather than refreshing later, so rerunning a campaign means picking the audience fresh.

## Compliance basics

Every email campaign includes an unsubscribe link handled for you. The review step shows exactly who receives it before anything goes out.

<Note>
  A good rule of thumb: monthly at most, and only when there's a reason. Shops that respect the inbox keep their lists responsive; shops that hammer them train customers to ignore everything.
</Note>
