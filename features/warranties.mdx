---
title: "Warranties issued from completed jobs"
description: "ShopWrk issues a warranty when you complete an eligible job service. Coverage is locked to that customer, vehicle, and job."
---

When you mark a job complete, ShopWrk issues a warranty for each eligible service on that job. You do not start on the Warranties page and type it in by hand.

One warranty per eligible service. If coverage is for a specific panel, you get one warranty for that panel. Completing the same job twice does not create a second copy.

## When a warranty is issued

A service is eligible when it has an active warranty profile in your catalog. Completing the job is what issues the warranty.

ShopWrk copies these onto the warranty at that moment:

- Customer
- Vehicle
- Job
- Service, or the panel when coverage is panel-specific
- Coverage length and terms from the warranty profile

The job has to be complete. Work that is still in progress does not get a warranty yet.

If the service itself is covered, you get one warranty for that service, not extra warranties for each panel on the same line. You only get a panel warranty when the service as a whole is not covered and that panel is.

If you later edit the profile or the catalog, issued warranties keep the original terms. The customer is covered for what you sold that day, not for a rewrite you made a year later.

<Note>
  If two people mark the same job complete at the same time, you still get one warranty per eligible service, not two.
</Note>

## How it connects to jobs and customers

The warranty sits on the same chain as the work. It belongs to that customer, that vehicle, and that completed job.

If the customer sells the car, coverage stays with the vehicle and the original job. It does not follow the person onto a different car.

Open **Warranties** in the sidebar to review what has already been issued. That list is for looking up coverage and claims. It is not where completed-job warranties are created.

## Adding a warranty yourself

You can still add a warranty by hand when automatic issue does not apply. Same rules: the job must be complete, the service must be eligible, and you still get one warranty per eligible service or panel.

## Claims

If a customer comes back with a problem, open the issued warranty and log a claim. Attach photos there. The terms you check against are the ones locked in at completion, even if you have changed the profile since then.
