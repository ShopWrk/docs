The Team area covers the employment loop: who works for you, when they work, what time they actually worked, what they can access, and what it all costs. Open **Team** in the sidebar. Tabs include **Staff**, **Schedules**, **Timesheet**, **Access**, and **Payroll**.

## Staff

The Staff tab lists everyone on your roster: active members, pending invitations, and deactivated accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a team member">
    Click **Invite member**, enter their name and email, and pick a role. They get an email invitation and show as Invited until they accept. Owner invitations expire after 24 hours; other roles get 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up their profile">
    Once active, each member has a profile covering role and access, contact details, schedule, tracked time, and compensation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Roles

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing, permissions, and shop settings |
| Admin | Day-to-day operations; no owner-only settings by default |
| Technician | Their assigned jobs, their own schedule, and their own time |

Roles are starting points. Owners can adjust any member's access with permission groups and individual overrides, so two people with similar jobs can see different things. If a control is missing for someone, an Owner can fix it from their Access settings.

Members can also belong to more than one shop. Switching happens from the account menu, which matters if you run a second location or a mobile unit.

## Schedules

Scheduling is week-by-week and shift-based:

- **Publish dated shifts.** Set each member's shifts for the coming week and publish them. The calendar shows staffing against booked jobs so you can spot an overbooked Tuesday before it happens.
- **Time off.** Staff request time off from their own view; you approve or decline from Schedules. Approved days feed assignment warnings instead of silently double-booking someone who is away.
- **Assignment warnings.** Assign a tech outside their published shifts or during approved time off and the calendar tells you before you save.

## Timesheet

The Timesheet tab shows real worked hours, not just scheduled ones:

- **Work sessions.** Clock in and out with breaks recorded as they happen. Corrections are logged, so payroll disputes have a paper trail.
- **Job timers.** Technicians log time against specific jobs while working them, feeding both labor costing and per-service commission calculations.
- **Weekly review.** Each member's week shows scheduled versus actual. Missing clock-ins are flagged rather than quietly becoming zero hours.

<Tip>
  Review timesheets midweek, not on payday. A missed clock-out is a ten-second fix Wednesday and an argument Friday.
</Tip>

## Payroll prep

The Payroll tab compiles each member's approved hours at their rate plus commissions earned on completed jobs, ready to hand to your payroll process.

Commissions follow your compensation setup: per-service rates assigned to the technician who did that line of work. Configure rates on each member's compensation settings, and the ledger builds itself as jobs complete.

<Note>
  ShopWrk handles payroll preparation. The final pay run, tax withholding, and paystub delivery happen in your payroll provider. Export the prep numbers and close the loop there.
</Note>
