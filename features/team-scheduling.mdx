The Team area covers the whole employment loop: who works for you, when each of them works, what time they actually worked, and what that time costs. Everything lives under **Team** in the sidebar, across four tabs: **Directory**, **Schedules**, **Time**, and **Payroll**.

## Directory

The directory lists every member of your shop: active members, pending invitations, and deactivated accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a team member">
    Click **Invite**, enter their name and email, and pick a role. They get an email invitation and show as Invited until they accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up their profile">
    Once active, each member has a profile with their role and access, contact details, schedule, tracked time, and compensation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Roles

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing, payroll, and shop settings |
| Admin | Day-to-day operations; no owner-only settings or payroll |
| Technician | Their assigned jobs, their own schedule, and their own time |

Members can also belong to more than one shop. Switching shops happens from the account menu, which matters for owners running multiple locations or a mobile unit alongside the main shop.

## Schedules

Scheduling is week-by-week and shift-based, built for shops where hours genuinely change:

- **Publish dated shifts.** Set each member's shifts for the coming week and publish them. The calendar shows staffing against booked jobs so you can spot an overbooked Tuesday before it happens.
- **Recurring patterns.** Save a typical week as a template and apply it in seconds instead of rebuilding every Sunday night.
- **Time off.** Staff request time off from their own view; you approve or decline from Schedules. Approved days block job assignment warnings for that person rather than silently double-booking them.
- **Assignment warnings.** When you assign a tech to a job outside their published shifts or during approved time off, the calendar tells you before you save.

## Time tracking

The Time tab shows real worked hours, not just scheduled ones:

- **Work sessions.** Clock in and out with breaks recorded as they happen. Edits and corrections are logged, so payroll disputes have a paper trail.
- **Job timers.** Technicians log time against specific jobs while working them, feeding both labor costing and per-service commission calculations.
- **Weekly review.** Each member's week appears as scheduled versus actual. Missing clock-ins are flagged rather than quietly becoming zero hours.

<Tip>
  Review the Time tab midweek, not on payday. A missed clock-out is a ten-second fix Wednesday and an argument Friday.
</Tip>

## Payroll

Payroll turns approved time into money owed. The flow is review first, then run:

1. **Pick the period.** Confirm the pay period dates.
2. **Review payroll prep.** ShopWrk compiles each member's approved hours at their hourly rate plus any commissions earned on completed jobs. Adjust anything wrong here; corrections after the run are messier.
3. **Run payroll.** Finalize the run. Every member gets a paystub saved to their profile.

Commissions follow your compensation setup: per-service rates assigned to the technician who did that line of work. Configure rates on each member's Compensation tab, and the ledger builds itself as jobs complete.

<Warning>
  A finalized payroll run cannot be undone. Check hours and rates carefully before running.
</Warning>
