Staff is your people. Schedules is the dated week you publish. Timesheet is who clocked in. Access is permission groups. Payroll Prep is hourly snapshots and a CSV. How each person is paid lives on their profile under **Compensation**. There is no **Run Payroll** button.

Anyone with permission to view the team can open Team. Tabs they cannot use stay hidden. Compensation, payroll prep, and a few other actions need extra grants. Approving a snapshot and exporting the CSV stay with an Owner.

For permission groups, the locked Administrator group, and who can do what, see [Roles and permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions).

## Staff

Staff is the shop directory. Active people, invited people, and deactivated people sit in the same table. There is no separate pending-invitations list.

Invited people show status **Invited**. Filter the table to **Status = Invited** when you want only those rows. An invited row is not a profile link. They get a profile after they accept.

### Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Staff">
    Go to **Team → Staff**. Invite is on the Staff tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Invite Team Member">
    Click **Invite Team Member**. Email, Name, and Phone are all required. The invite also has a Role field. The tooltip says you can change this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Send it. They stay **Invited** in the Staff table until they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ShopWrk emails them a join link. Phone is required. That number becomes their **Work Phone** for this shop only. ShopWrk calling and texts in this shop use that Work Phone, not their personal sign-in phone.

- **Profile** holds name, phone, and shop membership details. You can view their sign-in email when you are allowed to. You cannot change it for them. They change their own email.
- **Access** is this person's groups. See [Roles and permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions).
- **Schedule** shows this person's dated shifts and time off. Manage shifts from **Team → Schedules**.
- **Stats** shows jobs completed, paid hours this week versus last week, and time per service.
- **Activity** is the audit trail for membership and access changes.
- **Compensation** is how this person is paid: **Hourly**, **Percentage**, or **Fixed fee**, plus **Pay history**.

You can deactivate or reactivate a member from the profile. Deactivation cancels future draft and published shifts, keeps history, and notifies the people who need to know. Reactivating restores their previous groups and overrides.

<Note>
  Stats hours come from paid work sessions, not job timers. Job time is operational. It is not pay.
</Note>

## Compensation

Compensation is where you set how each team member is paid and keep a history of those terms. It lives on the member profile, not on Payroll Prep.

You pick one method: **Hourly**, **Percentage**, or **Fixed fee**. You can save percentage and fixed fee today. Payroll Prep still only calculates hourly. Those other methods are stored for later. They are not payable in Payroll Prep yet.

If you have permission to view or manage compensation, open the member and go to **Compensation**. People without that grant do not see the tab. Viewing your own profile does not unlock it. Technicians without compensation permission cannot see rates, including their own.

Salary is not a method you can pick. Paystubs, direct deposit, and **Run Payroll** are not on this tab.

### Compensation vs Timesheet vs Payroll Prep

Use Compensation for pay method and amount. Use Timesheet for who was in the shop. Use Payroll Prep only for hourly export.

| What you want | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Set hourly, percentage, or fixed fee, and keep history | Member **Compensation**, **Pay** section |
| Who clocked in | **Team → Timesheet** |
| Hourly snapshot and CSV | **Team → Payroll Prep** |
| Time on a specific car | Job timers on the [job](/workflows/jobs). Not pay. |

Invites stay on **Team → Staff**. Job timers never become pay.

A successful save shows **Pay settings saved**. Changing the hourly rate can also show **Hourly rate saved**. If you switch this person off hourly while a shift is open, you also see **Their open shift was closed for review**.

### Walkthrough: wrap installer on hourly, then a share of sales

Luis installs wraps at Maya's shop. He has been treated as hourly because nobody saved a method yet.

1. Maya goes to **Team → Staff**, opens Luis, then **Compensation**.
2. Under **Pay**, she leaves **Pay method** on **Hourly**, enters **Hourly rate** $28.00, and clicks **Save changes**. The toast says **Pay settings saved**. **Pay history** shows a current hourly row.
3. After a busy wrap week she switches **Pay method** to **Percentage** and enters 40. He is still clocked in from the morning. Save shows **Pay settings saved**, then **Their open shift was closed for review**.
4. She opens [Payroll Prep](#payroll-prep) for that period. ShopWrk will not calculate or approve Luis on percentage. She can leave the stored 40% for later, or switch him back to hourly if she needs this period's CSV.

The 40% is the usual share. If one wrap job should pay a different share, that job can set its own rate. Payroll Prep still will not export percentage or fixed fee.

### Compensation gotchas

- **Hourly implies the shift clock.** There is no attendance switch on this tab.
- **Leaving hourly with an open shift closes it for review.** Check [Timesheet](#timesheet) before you need a snapshot.
- **Unconfigured is treated as hourly** until you save. That is not the same as a saved rate. A missing hourly rate for payable time still blocks Payroll Prep.
- **Percentage and fixed fee are not in Payroll Prep yet.** You can configure them. You cannot approve or export a period that includes those methods.
- **Scheduled terms lock the form** until that date.
- **Compensation is a grant.** View Compensation and Manage Compensation are separate. See [Roles and permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions).
- **Salary is not a method** in **Pay method**. Paystubs, direct deposit, and **Run Payroll** are not here.

## Schedules

**Team → Schedules** shows dated **Staff shifts** first, then repeating weekly availability, then time off.

Staff shifts are the real week you publish. The header shows Sunday through Saturday dates. Use **Jump to week** to move around.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the week">
    Select a team member's cell for the day, then click **Add shift**. Enter the start and end time, then save. **Copy last week** pulls the previous week's dated shifts when that saves time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish week">
    Click **Publish week** when the week is ready. People get in-app and email notices for publish and later material changes. They do not have to acknowledge the shift.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeating availability is the usual Sunday through Saturday pattern. It is not a dated shift. **Publish week** is the plan staff should follow that week.

### Time off

Staff can request full-day or partial-day time off. An Owner, or someone with Manage Schedule, approves it.

Time off never creates paid hours. If approved time off overlaps a published shift, resolve those shifts before you approve. ShopWrk will not leave a published shift sitting on top of approved time off.

## Timesheet

**Team → Timesheet** is shop-wide attendance. Staff can clock in from here. People with Manage Time can correct time.

The Staff table also has a **Job hours** column. That is time on jobs. Keep it distinct from paid attendance. Job timers are not what Payroll Prep pays.

## Access

**Team → Access** is where you set who can do what. See [Roles and permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions).

## Payroll Prep

**Team → Payroll Prep** builds hourly snapshots you can check and export. It does not pay people. There is no **Run Payroll** button, and ShopWrk does not claim tax or legal compliance.

Payroll Prep remains hourly-only. You can configure **Percentage** and **Fixed fee** on [Compensation](#compensation). A period that includes those methods cannot be approved or exported yet. The saved terms are ready for later. They are not payable today.

What a snapshot includes:

- Regular hours and the hourly rate segments used in the period
- Regular gross, overtime you classify, overtime gross, audited adjustments, and total gross
- Periods you configure as weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, or monthly

If a rate changes mid-period, ShopWrk splits the hours across the old and new rates. A missing hourly rate for payable time blocks the snapshot. Open or overlapping attendance blocks it too. Schedule mismatches warn you. They do not stop the snapshot.

Preparing a draft is a grant you can give. Approving a snapshot and exporting the CSV are Owner-only, and those are separate actions.

After an Owner approves a snapshot, that version is locked. If attendance, rates, overtime classification, or an adjustment changes, ShopWrk invalidates it and you prepare a new version. Old or invalidated versions cannot export.

The CSV is a full replacement for that approved version. Export it again and you get the same file.

Staff can see only their own approved gross. They do not see rates, other people's amounts, or draft snapshots.

<Warning>
  Payroll Prep is not **Run Payroll**. You can set percentage and fixed fee on Compensation now. Payroll Prep will not calculate or export those methods yet. Salary is not a pay method you can pick. Paystubs, direct deposit, and processing pay are still coming.
</Warning>

## Jobs and Work Phone

The people on Staff are who you assign on a job. On the job, job timers are operational time. They are not pay. Paid hours come from **Timesheet**. How that person is paid is on [Compensation](#compensation). Percentage and fixed fee defaults can differ on a given job.

When you assign a technician, ShopWrk can warn you about an availability conflict next to **Labor start**, **Work starts**, or the technician picker. Open the warning to see the reason.

If you have permission to manage the schedule, you can continue by entering a short override reason. ShopWrk saves that reason with the assignment.

Typical reasons:

- After hours
- No published shift
- Time off
- Overlap with another appointment in this shop

Overnight jobs that still start and end inside shop hours do not warn. Two jobs in the same bay at the same time still refuse, as do blocked time and a busy Google Calendar. Cross-shop schedule conflicts are ignored on purpose.

ShopWrk calling and texts for this shop use each person's **Work Phone** from the invite.

## What is not live yet

Payroll processing, paystubs, and direct deposit are not available. Salary is not a selectable pay method. You can configure **Hourly**, **Percentage**, and **Fixed fee** on the member **Compensation** tab. Payroll Prep still only calculates hourly. Use hourly compensation and Payroll Prep snapshots when you need numbers for your accountant or payroll provider this period.
