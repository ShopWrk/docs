Open **Contacts** for every customer and lead. Open **CRM** for the deal board when you want to move a sale through stages.

## Contacts

Everyone you work with lives in Contacts, whether they already booked or just inquired. Open **Contacts** from the sidebar to browse, search, and filter.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Customers" icon="user-check" href="/features/crm#contacts">
    Contacts with at least one completed job. Job history, invoices, and lifetime spend show on the profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Leads" icon="user-plus" href="/features/crm#contacts">
    Contacts who have not booked yet. Track source, status, and who owns the follow-up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Contact profile

Click a contact to open their profile. You will see:

- **Overview** shows name, phone, email, address, source, tags, and notes.
- **Vehicles** lists every vehicle on file with year, make, model, color, VIN, and plate.
- **Jobs** is the full job history with status, date, and services.
- **Estimates** lists open and closed estimates for this contact.
- **Invoices** shows payment status and overdue flags.
- **Deals** lists CRM deals linked to this contact.

<Tip>
  Filter contacts by tag, vehicle make, last visit, minimum job count, or minimum spend when you want high-value customers for a follow-up offer.
</Tip>

<Card title="Website visits from your site" icon="globe" href="/integrations/site-tracker">
After they submit a ShopWrk form on your website, open this contact and look at the activity feed. You will see **Website visit** rows for the pages they viewed. Copy the script from **Settings → Integrations → Site Tracker** and paste it on your WordPress, Shopify, or Wix site.
</Card>

## Lead pipeline

The CRM board is a set of columns. Each column is a stage in your sales process. Open **CRM** in the sidebar.

New shops get a default pipeline. Rename stages, add stages, or reorder them from **CRM → Settings → Pipelines**.

### Deals

Each card on the board is a deal: a potential sale tied to a contact, sitting in one stage at a time.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Creating a deal">
    Click **+ Add Deal** in any column. Enter the title, estimated value, linked contact, and notes. The card appears in that column.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Moving a deal">
    Drag the card to another column, or open the deal and change the stage. ShopWrk logs the change on the contact's activity timeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Closing a deal">
    Mark a deal **Won** or **Lost** from the detail panel. Won deals can trigger automations such as a thank-you text or referral follow-up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Leads list

Go to **CRM → Leads** for a table of all leads. Filter by status, source, and assignee. Use this when you want to work a list instead of dragging cards on the board.

## Tags

Tags are short labels on contacts for filtering and segments. Common examples: `VIP`, `Window Tint`, `PPF`, `Referral`, `Follow Up`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open tag settings">
    Go to **CRM → Settings → Tags**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a tag">
    Click **Add Tag**, enter a name, pick a color, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply to a contact">
    Open a contact profile and select tags in the Tags field on Overview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Tags created in CRM settings apply across Contacts. Filter by tag from the contacts table, and use tags in automations (for example, send a text when you tag someone VIP).
</Note>

## Pipeline settings

Go to **CRM → Settings** to configure pipelines and tags.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Pipelines">
    Create separate pipelines for different workflows (for example retail vs fleet). Set a default pipeline so that board opens when you go to CRM.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tags">
    Add, rename, recolor, or delete tags. Deleting a tag removes it from every contact that had it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
