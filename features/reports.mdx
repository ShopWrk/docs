Reports turn the work already flowing through ShopWrk into answers. How much did we bill this month? Which service makes the most money? Are customers paying on time? Which ads produce booked jobs? Every chart pulls live from your jobs, invoices, payments, and conversations, so there is nothing to export or re-enter.

Open **Reports** from the sidebar.

## The date range does the thinking

Every report starts with a period selector: this week, this month, this quarter, year to date, or a custom range. Pick a range once and every widget on the page follows it, so a single glance tells a consistent story instead of mixing numbers from different periods.

<Tip>
  Compare against the previous period when you change something, like a price increase or a new ad campaign. The delta tells you whether it worked better than any absolute number can.
</Tip>

## Questions these reports answer

- **Which service actually pays?** Revenue by service shows what a full front PPF brings in versus a basic tint job, including add-on attach rates.
- **Where do leads die?** The pipeline funnel shows how many quotes were sent, approved, deposited, and completed, so you can see if the leak is at quoting or at deposit collection.
- **Who owes me money?** Outstanding balances by age, with the invoice one click away for a reminder.
- **Is my ad spend working?** Leads by source connects campaign spend to booked revenue rather than raw form fills.
- **Are agents pulling their weight?** AI usage and outcomes show conversations handled, bookings made, and deposits collected on autopilot.

## Exporting

Any report exports to CSV from the toolbar. Owners usually export monthly close numbers for their accountant and leave the live view for day-to-day decisions.

<Note>
  Numbers on dashboards are computed from live data. If a job was just marked complete or an invoice just paid, give it a moment before presenting to a room; a refresh updates everything.
</Note>
