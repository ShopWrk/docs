## Voice receptionist

When a call comes in and nobody can pick up, the voice receptionist answers in your shop's style. It can answer common questions, check whether someone is already a customer, take a message, or book a time.

Calls it handles show up in [Conversations](/features/communications) with a transcript. Set how after-hours calls get handled with [Call Flow](/integrations/sms-voice#call-flow).

## Meta Ads agent

This agent connects your ad spend to booked, paid jobs. It can show cost per booked job, which creatives actually convert, and propose budget or creative changes. Proposals wait in an approvals queue. Nothing changes on your ad account until you approve it.

## What every agent reads

Keep these current. Every agent picks up the next conversation from the same place.

| What | Where you keep it |
| --- | --- |
| Services and vehicle-tier pricing | [Service catalog](/settings/services-catalog) |
| Calendar and availability | [Calendar and booking](/features/calendar-booking) |
| Customer history | [CRM](/features/crm) |
| Policies and FAQs | Knowledge under **AI**. See [AI assistant](/features/ai-assistant) |
| Tone and escalation | Agent settings under **Agents** |

## Guardrails

- Low confidence or a sensitive topic hands the conversation to a person, with a summary of what happened.
- Money-moving and ad-account changes wait for you unless you turn that on for that agent.
- Customers who reply STOP stop getting automated texts.
- One shop's data never leaks into another's answers.
