ShopWrk agents work from your services, prices, calendar, and conversations. They are not generic chatbots.

Open **Agents** in the sidebar to see them. The chat that helps *you* run the shop is **AI**.

## Voice receptionist

**Coming soon.** Not available yet. When it ships, it will answer calls the shop cannot pick up. Until then, use [Call Flow](/integrations/sms-voice#call-flow) so a person or voicemail handles inbound calls.

## Meta Ads agent

**Coming soon.** Not available yet. Connecting Instagram, Messenger, and Facebook Lead Ads is separate and works today. See [Instagram and Messenger](/integrations/social-media).

## What every agent reads

Keep these current. Every agent picks up the next conversation from the same place.

| What | Where you keep it |
| --- | --- |
| Services and vehicle-tier pricing | [Service catalog](/settings/services-catalog) |
| Calendar and availability | [Calendar and booking](/features/calendar-booking) |
| Customer history | [CRM](/features/crm) |
| Policies and FAQs | Knowledge under **AI**. See [AI assistant](/features/ai-assistant) |
| Tone and escalation | Agent settings under **Agents** |

## Guardrails

- Low confidence or a sensitive topic hands the conversation to a person, with a summary of what happened.
- Money-moving changes wait for you unless you turn that on for that agent.
- Customers who reply STOP stop getting automated texts.
- One shop's data never leaks into another's answers.
